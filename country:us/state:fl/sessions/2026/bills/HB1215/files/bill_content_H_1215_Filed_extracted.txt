HB 1215 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to public records and meetings;
3 creating s. 461.0182, F.S.; providing an exemption
4 from public records requirements for certain
5 information held by the Department of Health or the
6 Board of Podiatric Medicine pursuant to the Interstate
7 Podiatric Medical Licensure Compact; authorizing the
8 disclosure of such information under certain
9 circumstances; providing an exemption from public
10 meetings requirements for certain meetings or portions
11 of certain meetings of the Interstate Podiatric
12 Medical Licensure Compact Commission or its executive
13 committee or other committees; providing an exemption
14 from public records requirements for recordings,
15 minutes, and records generated during the exempt
16 meetings or portions of such meetings; providing for
17 future legislative review and repeal of the
18 exemptions; providing statements of public necessity;
19 providing a contingent effective date.
20
21 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

52 Section 2. (1) The Legislature finds that it is a public
53 necessity that a podiatric physician's personal identifying
54 information, other than the podiatric physicians name, licensure
55 status, or licensure number, obtained from the coordinated
56 information system, as described in Article VIII of s. 461.0181,
57 Florida Statutes, and held by the Department of Health or the
58 Board of Podiatric Medicine be made exempt from s. 119.07(1),
59 Florida Statutes, and s. 24(a), Article I of the State
60 Constitution. Protection of such information is required under
61 the Interstate Podiatric Medical Licensure Compact, which a
62 state must adopt in order to become a member state of the
63 compact. Without the public records exemption, this state will
64 be unable to effectively implement and administer the compact.
