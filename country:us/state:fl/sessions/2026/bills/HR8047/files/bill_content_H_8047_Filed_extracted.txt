HR 8047 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating February 14, 2026, as
3 "Ukrainian Festival Day" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, immigrants from Ukraine have come to the United
6 States for the opportunity to work and prosper in freedom and
7 more than 67,000 Ukrainian residents have made their home in
8 this state, and
9 WHEREAS, members of the Ukrainian-American community have
10 made a lasting impact in this state through their contributions
11 to the arts, culture, entertainment, historic and religious
12 institutions and services, and
13 WHEREAS, as a cultural staple in Orlando, the Ukrainian
14 Festival is a symbol of community, recognizing this state's
15 ethnic diversity and celebrating the Ukrainian heritage in
16 happiness, peace, and harmony, and
17 WHEREAS, many Ukrainian Americans maintain strong ties to
18 the people of Ukraine and deeply desire peace, justice, and
19 prosperity for their beloved home country, NOW, THEREFORE,
20
21 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of
22 Florida:
23
24 That February 14, 2026, is designated as "Ukrainian
25 Festival Day" in Florida.
