1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to school safety equipment; amending
3 s. 1001.212, F.S.; revising the purpose of specified
4 grants awarded by the Office of Safe Schools;
5 amending ss. 1002.42 and 1006.07, F.S.; providing
6 definitions; requiring private schools and public
7 schools, including charter schools, to have specified
8 safety equipment on each campus of the school;
9 requiring the Department of Education, in coordination
10 with the Department of Law Enforcement, to establish
11 minimum specifications for such equipment; authorizing
12 schools to use specified funds to meet the
13 requirements of such safety equipment; providing
14 training requirements for such safety equipment;
15 requiring schools to report specified information to
16 the Office of Safe Schools; authorizing the office to
17 conduct random audits for specified purposes;
18 providing an effective date.
