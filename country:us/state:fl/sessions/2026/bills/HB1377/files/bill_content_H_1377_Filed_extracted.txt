80 Section 1. Part III of chapter 386, Florida Statutes,
81 consisting of ss. 386.301-386.3101, Florida Statutes, is created
82 and entitled "Water Safety for Cooling Towers, Domestic Water
83 Holding Tanks, and Supplemental Disinfection Systems."
84 Section 2. Section 386.301, Florida Statutes, is created
85 to read:
86 386.301 Legislative intent.—The purpose of this part is to
87 protect people from the health hazards of Legionella, a
88 bacterium that is known to originate in improperly sanitized
89 cooling towers, domestic water holding tanks, and building
90 piping systems.
