F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 865 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to local regulation of drinking straws
3 and stirrers; providing legislative findings and
4 intent; creating s. 403.7034, F.S.; defining terms;
5 prohibiting local governmental entities from enacting
6 any rule, regulation, or ordinance for drinking straws
7 or stirrers which does not meet specified
8 requirements; providing requirements for local
9 governmental entities that elect to enact rules,
10 regulations, or ordinances for drinking straws or
11 stirrers; providing applicability; providing
12 construction; providing a directive to the Division of
13 Law Revision; providing an effective date.
14
15 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
16
17 Section 1. (1) The Legislature finds that:
18 (a) Many businesses and communities in this state are
19 using paper drinking straws and stirrers as a purportedly better
20 option for public health and the environment. However,
21 independent university studies have shown that most paper straws
22 contain harmful PFAS chemicals, exposure to which is linked to
23 concerning health risks.
24 (b) Paper drinking straw regulations marginalize residents
25 with disabilities for whom paper straws are not an option.

47 (c) "Industrial compostable certified" means a product
48 certified by a certification body as industrially compostable.
