HR 8065 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating November 2026 as "Epilepsy
3 Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, epilepsy, a brain disorder characterized by
6 recurring and unprovoked seizures, can affect people of every
7 gender, race, and age at any time, and
8 WHEREAS, according to the United States Centers for Disease
9 Control and Prevention, people with epilepsy face serious
10 barriers to health care access in this country because compared
11 to those without epilepsy, people with epilepsy are less likely
12 to be able to afford medication, specialty care, dental care,
13 and vision care and are also less likely to find transportation
14 and health care providers and specialists, and
15 WHEREAS, epilepsy is the fourth most common neurological
16 disorder in the world, afflicting approximately 3 million adults
17 age 18 and older and 456,000 children age 17 and younger in the
18 United States, and
19 WHEREAS, more than 500,000 people with the disorder live in
20 Florida, the highest concentration of people with epilepsy in
21 the nation, and
22 WHEREAS, people with epilepsy are up to three times more
23 likely than the general population to suffer premature death,
