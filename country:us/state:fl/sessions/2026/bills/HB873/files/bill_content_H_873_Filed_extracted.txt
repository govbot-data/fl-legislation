1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to health care practitioner maternal
3 mental health continuing education requirements;
4 creating s. 456.63, F.S.; providing curriculum
5 requirements relating to maternal mental health for
6 the required continuing education hours for licensure
7 renewal; requiring a board, or the Department of
8 Health if there is no board, to periodically update
9 specified curriculum and accept, as credit toward
10 continuing education requirements, the successful
11 completion of such curriculum; providing an effective
12 date.
13
14 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
15
16 Section 1. Section 456.63, Florida Statutes, is created to
17 read:
18 456.63 Continuing education on maternal mental health.-
19 (1) Continuing education hours required for licensure
20 renewal for health care practitioners shall include all of the
21 following:
22 (a) The best practices in screening for maternal mental
23 health disorders.
24 (b) The range of maternal mental health disorders.
25 (c) The range of evidence-based treatment options,
