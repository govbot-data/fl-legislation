HB 1137 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to excise tax deductions on alcoholic
3 beverages; creating s. 561.1215, F.S.; requiring
4 certain distributors of alcoholic beverages to receive
5 compensation in the form of an excise tax deduction;
6 providing the amount of such deduction; providing a
7 specified calculation for malt beverages; providing
8 applicability; defining the term "extraordinary loss";
9 requiring distributors of alcoholic beverages to
10 notify the Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco
11 of certain extraordinary losses; requiring specified
12 proof and documentation to be submitted to the
13 division; authorizing the division to adopt rules;
14 providing an effective date.
15
16 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
17

19 to read:
