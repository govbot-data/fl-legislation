F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 885 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to transportation facility
3 designations; providing an honorary designation of a
4 certain transportation facility in a specified county;
5 directing the Department of Transportation to erect
6 suitable markers; providing an effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. State Representative/Circuit Judge Hon. John J.
11 Crews, Jr., Memorial Highway designated; Department of
12 Transportation to erect suitable markers.—
13 (1) That portion of U.S. 90 between the intersection of
14 North 3rd Street in Baker County and the Nassau County line is
15 designated as "State Representative/Circuit Judge Hon. John J.
16 Crews, Jr., Memorial Highway."
17 (2) The Department of Transportation is directed to erect
18 suitable markers designating State Representative/Circuit Judge
19 Hon. John J. Crews, Jr., Memorial Highway as described in
20 subsection (1).
21 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
