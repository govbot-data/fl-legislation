1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating November 2026 as "Lung Cancer
3 Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, lung cancer is the leading cause of cancer-related
6 deaths in the United States and in Florida, claiming more lives
7 each year than breast, prostate, and colorectal cancers
8 combined, and
9 WHEREAS, lung cancer affects individuals and families of
10 all ages, backgrounds, and cultures, and many individuals
11 diagnosed with lung cancer have never smoked tobacco or had quit
12 smoking decades before the diagnosis, and
13 WHEREAS, early detection of lung cancer through evidence-
14 based screening, advancements in treatment, and increased public
15 awareness have significantly improved survival rates and the
16 quality of life for lung cancer patients, and
17 WHEREAS, Lung Cancer Awareness Month, observed nationally
18 each November, serves to educate the public about the risk
19 factors and symptoms of, and screening options for, lung cancer
20 and the importance of lung cancer prevention, research, and
21 medical innovation, and
22 WHEREAS, recognizing Lung Cancer Awareness Month honors
23 those who have lost their lives to the disease, supports
24 patients currently battling lung cancer, and acknowledges the
