29 766.318 Civil liability for provision of sex-reassignment

31 (5)(a) The Attorney General may conduct an investigation

39 accrue to the benefit of the injured minor.
40 (c) This subsection does not apply to:
41 1. Treatment with sex-reassignment prescriptions if such
