31 Section 1. Section 320.021, Florida Statutes, is created

33 320.021 Blue envelope program.—
34 (1) The department shall establish a blue envelope program
35 for the purpose of improving communication between individuals
36 with autism spectrum disorder and law enforcement officers
37 during motor vehicle-related interactions.
38 (2) By January 1, 2027, the department shall develop and
39 make available to individuals with autism spectrum disorder a
40 blue envelope that is intended to hold a copy of an individual's
41 driver license and his or her vehicle registration, proof of
42 insurance, and emergency contact information, which envelope may
43 be provided by the individual to a law enforcement officer
44 during a motor vehicle-related interaction. The exterior of the
45 blue envelope must identify the individual as an individual with
46 autism spectrum disorder and include communication guidelines
47 intended to assist law enforcement officers during interactions
48 with drivers with autism spectrum disorder.
