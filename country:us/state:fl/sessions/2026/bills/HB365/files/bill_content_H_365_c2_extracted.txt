43 proof of insurance, and emergency contact information, and a
44 blue card, which envelope or card may be provided by an
45 individual with autism spectrum disorder to a law enforcement
46 officer during an interaction with the law enforcement officer.
47 (b) The exterior of the blue envelope must identify the
48 individual as an individual with autism spectrum disorder and
49 include communication guidelines intended to assist law
50 enforcement officers during interactions with drivers with
