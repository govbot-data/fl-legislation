HB 1525 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to lewd or lascivious exhibition;
3 amending s. 800.04, F.S.; defining the term
4 "presence"; providing that a person commits lewd or
5 lascivious exhibition when the person commits certain
6 acts in the presence of a victim who is less than 16
7 years of age and the person knows or has reason to
8 know such victim is present; providing penalties;
9 providing an effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Paragraphs (d) and (e) of subsection (1) of
14 section 800.04, Florida Statutes, are redesignated as paragraphs
15 (e) and (f), respectively, paragraph (a) of subsection (7) is
16 amended, a new paragraph (d) is added to subsection (1), and
17 paragraphs (b) and (c) of subsection (7) of that section are
18 republished, to read:
19 800.04 Lewd or lascivious offenses committed upon or in
20 the presence of persons less than 16 years of age.—
21 (1) DEFINITIONS.—As used in this section:
22 (d) "Presence" means a state in which the victim is
23 reasonably capable of viewing the specified conduct. The term
24 does not require that a victim is aware of any specific conduct
25 and does not require that a victim sees or perceives the

27 (7) LEWD OR LASCIVIOUS EXHIBITION.—
