1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to public records; amending s.

6 investigators licensed by the Department of
7 Agriculture and Consumer Services and the spouses and
8 children of such private investigators; providing for
9 future legislative review and repeal of the exemption;
10 providing a statement of public necessity; providing
11 an effective date.

51 private investigators often interact with individuals who have
52 strong motives to retaliate or cause harm. Public access to a
53 private investigator's home address or personal information

55 threats, harassment, stalking, or physical violence. Protecting
