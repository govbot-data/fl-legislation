51 Section 1. This act may be cited as the "Northeast Florida
52 Rivers, Springs, and Community Investment Act."
53 Section 2. Section 373.464, Florida Statutes, is created
54 to read:
55 373.464 Ocklawaha River restoration, recreation, and
56 economic development.—
57 (1) PROJECT LEAD.—The department shall, no later than
58 August 31, 2026, hire a full-time equivalent contractor or
59 employee, whose position title will be project lead, to oversee
60 the implementation of this act. The project lead must have
61 subject matter expertise in conservation and recreation
62 planning.
63 (2) PROJECT PLAN.—
64 (a) By July 1, 2027, the department shall develop a
65 project plan for the restoration of the Ocklawaha River. The
66 project plan must provide for restoration and increased
67 resiliency and recreation benefits of the Ocklawaha and St.
68 Johns Rivers and Silver Springs. The project plan shall be
69 designed to restore the Ocklawaha River hydrology and floodplain
70 function to the approximate conditions that existed prior to the
71 construction of the Cross Florida Barge Canal project and its
72 associated project works, including, but not limited to, the
73 Kirkpatrick Dam and Buckman Lock. The project plan shall be
74 based on the restoration design and steps set forth in the
75 department's pending Joint Application for Environmental
