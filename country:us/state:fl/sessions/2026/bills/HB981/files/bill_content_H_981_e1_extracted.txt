51 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
52
53 Section 1. This act may be cited as the "Northeast Florida
54 Rivers, Springs, and Community Investment Act."
55 Section 2. Section 373.464, Florida Statutes, is created
56 to read:
57 373.464 Ocklawaha River restoration, recreation, and
58 economic development.—
59 (1) PROJECT LEAD.—The department shall, no later than
60 August 31, 2026, hire a full-time equivalent contractor or
61 employee, whose position title will be project lead, to oversee
62 the implementation of this act. The project lead must have
63 subject matter expertise in conservation and recreation
64 planning.
65 (2) PROJECT PLAN.—
66 (a) By July 1, 2027, the department shall develop a
67 project plan for the restoration of the Ocklawaha River with the
68 goal of restoring the river's natural flow, hydrology, and
69 floodplain function to the approximate conditions that existed
70 before the construction of the Cross Florida Barge Canal project
71 and its associated project works. The project plan must provide
72 for restoration and increased resiliency and recreation benefits
73 of the Ocklawaha and St. Johns Rivers and Silver Springs. The
74 project plan must incorporate the best available science and
75 include engineering and design updates, including topographic
