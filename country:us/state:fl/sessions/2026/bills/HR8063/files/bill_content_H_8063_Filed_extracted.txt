HR 8063 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating October 15, 2026, as
3 "National Pregnancy and Infant Loss Remembrance Day"
4 in Florida.
5
6 WHEREAS, the loss of an infant is a devastating occurrence,
7 yet many parents and families feel they cannot talk openly about
8 such grief and trauma due to social and cultural taboos against
9 miscarriage, stillbirth, and medical complications, and
10 WHEREAS, in 1988, the United States Congress chose the
11 month of October as "Pregnancy and Infant Loss Awareness Month,"
12 and President Ronald Reagan subsequently issued a proclamation
13 observing this month, and
14 WHEREAS, on September 28, 2006, the United States Congress
15 passed Concurrent Resolution 222 supporting the goals and ideals
16 of National Pregnancy and Infant Loss Remembrance Day, and
17 WHEREAS, the United Nations Inter-agency Group for Child
18 Mortality Estimation, led by UNICEF, reported that, in 2023,
19 approximately 2.3 million newborns died in their first month of
20 life and that nearly 1.9 million stillbirths occurred globally,
21 equivalent to roughly one stillbirth every 17 seconds, and
22 WHEREAS, the American College of Obstetricians and
23 Gynecologists estimates miscarriage is the most common form of
24 pregnancy loss, with as many as 26 percent of all pregnancies
25 ending in miscarriage, and
