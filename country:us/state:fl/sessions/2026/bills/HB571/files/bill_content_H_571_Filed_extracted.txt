HB 571 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to motorcycle specialty license
3 plates; amending s. 320.08058, F.S.; requiring the
4 Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles to
5 develop an Endless Summer motorcycle specialty license
6 plate; specifying a design requirement for the plate;
7 requiring that the number of valid Endless Summer
8 motor vehicle and motorcycle specialty license plates
9 be added together for purposes of a certain
10 determination; providing an effective date.
11
12 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
13
14 Section 1. Subsection (66) of section 320.08058, Florida
15 Statutes, is amended to read:
16 320.08058 Specialty license plates.—
17 (66) ENDLESS SUMMER LICENSE PLATES.—
18 (a) The department shall develop an Endless Summer license
19 plate for display on a motor vehicle and for display on a
20 motorcycle as provided in this section. Endless Summer license
21 plates must bear the colors and design approved by the
22 department. The word "Florida" must appear at the top of the
23 plate, and the words "Endless Summer" must appear at the bottom
24 of the plate. Any department-approved image must be placed on
25 the far left side of a plate for display on a motorcycle.
