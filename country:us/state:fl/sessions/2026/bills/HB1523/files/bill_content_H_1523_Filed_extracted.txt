HB 1523 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to food waste reduction; creating s.
3 1013.121, F.S.; requiring the Department of Education
4 to develop a specified policy; requiring school
5 districts to adopt the policy within a specified
6 timeframe; providing requirements for the policy;
7 requiring each participating school district to submit
8 a report to the department by a specified date;
9 providing requirements for the report; providing an
10 effective date.
11
12 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
13
14 Section 1. Section 1013.121, Florida Statutes, is created
15 to read:
16 1013.121 School food and plastic waste reduction.—
17 (1) The Department of Education shall develop a policy to
18 be adopted by 25 percent of school districts in this state by
19 the beginning of the 2027-2028 school year and to be adopted by
20 100 percent of school districts in this state by the beginning
21 of the 2029-2030 school year, which requires all of the
22 following:
23 (a) A partnership with a local nonprofit organization or
24 food bank to redistribute edible cafeteria food and divert
25 organic food waste to a composting program.
