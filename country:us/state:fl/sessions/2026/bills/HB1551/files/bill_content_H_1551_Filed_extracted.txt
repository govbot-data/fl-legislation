F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 1551 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to products liability for firearms;
3 creating s. 768.1258, F.S.; providing definitions;
4 providing that a firearm manufacturer is not liable
5 for certain torts based on the absence or presence of
6 certain features, mechanisms, or standards not
7 required under federal law; providing construction;
8 providing an effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. Section 768.1258, Florida Statutes, is created
13 to read:
14 768.1258 Limitation of actions against firearm
15 manufacturers.—
16 (1) As used in this section, the term:
17 (a) "Authorized user recognition technology" means a
18 mechanism, device, or technology applied to a firearm which
19 prevents a user not authorized by the firearm owner from firing
20 the firearm.
21 (b) "External manual safety" means a manually operated
22 mechanism that, when engaged, blocks the firearm trigger from
23 functioning.
24 (c) "Firearm" has the same meaning as in s. 790.001.
25 (d) "Loaded chamber indicator" means a mechanism or

28 (e) "Magazine disconnect mechanism" means a mechanism that

31 (2) In a products liability action involving a firearm, a
32 firearm manufacturer is not liable under a theory of defective
33 product design, failure to warn, negligence, strict liability,
34 or other claims based on the absence or presence of any design
35 feature, functionality, safety mechanism, or performance

37 the lawful manufacture, sale, import, or receipt of the firearm,
38 including, but not limited to:
39 (a) A magazine disconnect mechanism;
40 (b) A loaded chamber indicator;
41 (c) Authorized user recognition technology; or
42 (d) An external manual safety, including, but not limited
43 to, a hinged, pivoting, or tabbed trigger safety.
44 (3) This section does not limit liability for a firearm
45 manufacturer in cases in which the claimant establishes that the
46 firearm contained an actual manufacturing defect or failed to
47 operate in a manner consistent with the manufacturer's express
48 warranties or representations.
49 Section 2. This act shall take effect upon becoming a law.
