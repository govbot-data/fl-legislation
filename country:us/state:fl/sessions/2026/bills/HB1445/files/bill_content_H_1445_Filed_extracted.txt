HB 1445 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to public records; creating s.
3 1004.43521, F.S.; providing a public records exemption
4 for specified patient information for the Parkinson's
5 disease registry; authorizing certain information to
6 be disclosed under certain circumstances; providing a
7 contingent effective date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11 Section 1. Section 1004.43521, Florida Statutes, is
12 created to read:
13 1004.43521 Parkinson's disease registry; public records
14 exemption.—
15 (1) Information held in the Parkinson's disease registry
16 under s. 1004.4352 which identifies a patient is confidential
17 and exempt from s. 119.07(1) and s. 24(a), Art. I of the State
18 Constitution.
19 (2) Such information may be disclosed to the following:
20 (a) The Parkinson's Disease Research Board.
21 (b) A private entity that the board has contracted with to
22 establish and maintain the registry if such entity agrees to:
23 1. Submit a research plan to the board which specifies the
24 exact nature of the information requested and the intended use
25 of the information;
