1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to towing undrivable vehicles;
3 creating s. 323.003, F.S.; providing requirements when
4 a motor vehicle is disabled due to a motor vehicle
5 crash; requiring a law enforcement officer and wrecker
6 operator to provide specified information to the
7 driver of the vehicle; requiring a wrecker operator
8 storage facility to provide certain notification to
9 the owner of the vehicle at specific intervals;
10 providing an effective date.
11
12 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
13
14 Section 1. Section 322.003, Florida Statutes, is created
15 to read:
16 323.003 Towing disabled vehicles; notification.—
17 (1) When a motor vehicle is disabled due to a motor
18 vehicle crash and a wrecker operator is needed to tow the
19 vehicle:
20 (a) If law enforcement is impounding the vehicle, a law
21 enforcement officer shall provide the driver with a list of
22 wrecker operators the driver may choose from; or
23 (b) The driver may call a wrecker operator of his or her
24 choice.
