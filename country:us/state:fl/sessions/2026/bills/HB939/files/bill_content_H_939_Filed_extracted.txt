28 Section 2. The Legislature finds that it is a public
29 necessity that e-mail addresses collected by the Department of
30 Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles for the use of e-mail in lieu
31 of the United States Postal Service as a method of notification
32 be made exempt from s. 119.07(1), Florida Statutes, and s.
33 24(a), Article I of the State Constitution. Sections 319.40(3),
34 320.95(2), and 322.08(10), Florida Statutes, authorize the
35 department to collect e-mail addresses and use e-mail in lieu of
36 the United States Postal Service to provide title certification
37 notification and renewal notices related to motor vehicle
38 license plates, driver licenses, and identification cards. The
39 department is also authorized to collect e-mail addresses and
40 use e-mail in lieu of the United States Postal Service to
41 provide renewal notices related to vessel registrations under s.
42 328.30(3), Florida Statutes. HB 937 expands the circumstances in
43 which e-mail addresses may be used in lieu of the United States
44 Postal Service by authorizing e-mail to be used as a method of
45 notification for various notices and orders issued by the
46 department in addition to renewal notices, including, but not
47 limited to, notices related to driver licenses, identification
48 cards, motor vehicle registrations, motor vehicle title
49 certificates, vessel registrations, and orders to revoke,
