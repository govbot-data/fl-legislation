1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to developmental disabilities;
3 amending s. 393.063, F.S.; revising the definition of

6 date.

8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. Subsections (46) and (47) of section 393.063,
11 Florida Statutes, are renumbered as subsections (47) and (48),
12 respectively, subsection (11) is amended, and a new subsection
13 (46) is added to that section, to read:
14 393.063 Definitions.—For the purposes of this chapter, the
15 term:
16 (11) "Developmental disability" means a disorder or
17 syndrome that is attributable to intellectual disability,
18 cerebral palsy, autism, spina bifida, Down syndrome, Phelan-
19 McDermid syndrome, or Prader-Willi syndrome, or Tatton-Brown-
20 Rahman syndrome; that manifests before the age of 18; and that
21 constitutes a substantial handicap that can reasonably be
22 expected to continue indefinitely.
23 (46) "Tatton-Brown-Rahman syndrome" means a disorder
24 caused by a mutation in the DNMT3A gene typified by mild to
25 severe intellectual disability, macrocephaly, stature and weight
