1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to voter registration and
3 identification; amending s. 97.053, F.S.; requiring
4 the supervisor of elections to verify an applicant's
5 citizenship status under certain circumstances and
6 refer certain matters to the Office of Election Crimes
7 and Security; amending s. 98.045, F.S.; providing
8 certain action is considered a written request from a
9 voter to have his or her name removed from the
10 statewide voter registration system; amending s.
11 101.043, F.S.; revising identifications accepted at
12 polling places; requiring that certain electors show a
13 Florida identification card upon entering the polling
14 place; providing that certain noncitizens may vote a
15 provisional ballot; amending s. 101.62, F.S.;
16 requiring the supervisor of elections to verify
17 certain information upon receiving a request for a
18 vote-by-mail ballot; amending s. 322.141, F.S.;
19 requiring certain licenses and identification cards to
20 denote certain persons as noncitizens; providing an
21 effective date.
