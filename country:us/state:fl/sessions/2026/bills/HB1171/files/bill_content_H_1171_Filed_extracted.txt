HB 1171 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to endangered or threatened marine
3 animals; providing a short title; creating s.
4 379.24312, F.S.; providing definitions; prohibiting
5 the collection and transportation of endangered or
6 threatened marine animals from state waters;
7 prohibiting the Fish and Wildlife Conservation
8 Commission from issuing, renewing, or approving
9 certain licenses; providing penalties; providing
10 construction; amending s. 379.401, F.S.; conforming a
11 provision to changes made by the act; providing an
12 effective date.
13
14 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
15
16 Section 1. This act may be cited as the "Marine At-Risk
17 Native Endangered or Threatened Animal Protection Act."
18 Section 2. Section 379.24312, Florida Statutes, is created
19 to read:
20 379.24312 Protections for endangered or threatened marine
21 animals.—
22 (1) As used in this section, the term:
23 (a) "Collect" means to physically capture, catch, remove,
24 or possess a marine animal species, or attempt to engage in any
25 such conduct.
