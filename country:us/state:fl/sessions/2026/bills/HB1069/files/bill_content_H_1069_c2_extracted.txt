1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to background screenings; amending s.
3 943.0438, F.S.; revising the definition of the term
4 "athletic coach"; providing that an independent
5 sanctioning authority is deemed to be a qualified
6 entity for the purpose of participating in the Care
7 Provider Background Screening Clearinghouse; amending
8 s. 943.0542, F.S.; requiring qualified entities to
9 designate a user administrator for a specified
10 purpose; amending ss. 943.0585 and 943.059, F.S.;
11 prohibiting certain persons from denying or failing to
12 acknowledge certain criminal history records that have
13 been expunged or sealed; requiring the Department of
14 Law Enforcement to disclose sealed criminal history
15 records under specified circumstances; reenacting ss.
16 943.053(3)(c), 943.0578(4), and 943.0582(2)(b), F.S.,
17 relating to dissemination of criminal justice
18 information, lawful self-defense expunction, and
19 diversion program expunction, respectively, to
20 incorporate the amendments made to ss. 943.0585 and
21 943.059, F.S., in references thereto; providing an
22 effective date.
