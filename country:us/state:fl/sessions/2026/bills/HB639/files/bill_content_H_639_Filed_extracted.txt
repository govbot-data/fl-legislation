HB 639 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to specialty license plates; amending
3 s. 320.08058, F.S.; revising provisions relating to
4 the issuance and fee distribution for Fraternal Order
5 of Police license plates; providing an effective date.
6
7 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
8
9 Section 1. Subsection (67) of section 320.08058, Florida
10 Statutes, is amended to read:
11 320.08058 Specialty license plates.—
12 (67) FRATERNAL ORDER OF POLICE LICENSE PLATES.—
13 (a)1. The department shall develop a Fraternal Order of
14 Police license plate as provided in this section. The plate must
15 bear the colors and design approved by the department. The word
16 "Florida" must appear at the top of the plate, and the words
17 "Fraternal Order of Police" must appear at the bottom of the
18 plate.
19 2. The department may issue the plate only to an applicant
20 who submits a notarized letter from the Florida State Lodge of
21 the Fraternal Order of Police stating that the applicant is a
22 member of the lodge in good standing or a member of a lodge
23 member's family, together with other fees and documents required
24 for a specialty plate.
25 (b) The annual use fees shall be distributed to the
