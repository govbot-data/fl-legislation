F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
CS/HB 1013 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to nurse registries; amending s.
3 400.506, F.S.; requiring each nurse registry to
4 display a specified disclaimer in a clear and
5 conspicuous manner on its website and all advertising
6 and marketing materials; providing an effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. Subsection (21) is added to section 400.506,
11 Florida Statutes, to read:
12 400.506 Licensure of nurse registries; requirements;
13 penalties.—
14 (21) Each nurse registry must display, in a clear and
15 conspicuous manner on the homepage of its website and on the
16 face of all digital or print marketing and advertising
17 materials, the following disclaimer:
18
19 "All caregivers referred by this nurse registry are
20 independent contractors of the client, not employees
21 of either the nurse registry or the client."
22
23 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
