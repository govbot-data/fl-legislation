CS/HB 4087 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Highlands County Hospital
3 District; amending chapter 2004-458, Laws of Florida;
4 providing requirements and duties of the Board of
5 Commissioners of the Highlands County Hospital
6 District before a lease or management agreement with
7 not-for-profit or for-profit corporations; providing
8 requirements for the agreement; providing
9 applicability; authorizing the board to sell the
10 entirety of the assets of the hospital to a not-for-
11 profit or for-profit entity under certain
12 circumstances; providing requirements and duties of
13 the board before the sale; providing requirements for
14 the sale agreement; requiring the hospital to file a
15 copy of the sale agreement with the Department of
16 Commerce; requiring the board to notify the department
17 of the sale; providing for judicial reviews; providing
18 for court costs; providing construction; providing an
19 effective date.
