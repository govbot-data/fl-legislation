HR 8103 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating May 10, 2026, as "First
3 Responders Mental Health Awareness Day" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, the first responders in this state risk their
6 lives each day to protect and assist the public, and
7 WHEREAS, first responders often experience high levels of
8 job-related stress, trauma, and exposure to life-threatening
9 situations that can have a profound impact on their mental
10 health and emotional well-being, and
11 WHEREAS, the mental health of first responders directly
12 affects their ability to make quick, sound decisions in high-
13 pressure situations, which in turn impacts public safety and
14 community trust, and
15 WHEREAS, the family members of first responders are also
16 affected by the stressors associated with a first responder's
17 job, and
18 WHEREAS, providing support for the families of first
19 responders is a key component of overall mental health and
20 wellness, and
21 WHEREAS, acknowledging and addressing the mental health
22 needs of first responders is essential to preserving their
23 health, safety, and ability to continue serving the residents of
24 this state, and
