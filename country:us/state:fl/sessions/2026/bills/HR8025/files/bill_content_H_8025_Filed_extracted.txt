1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating November 2026 as "Hindu-
3 American Heritage Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, there are around 1.2 billion Hindus worldwide and
6 approximately 219,916 Hindu Americans who live across Florida,
7 and
8 WHEREAS, Florida is enriched by the extraordinary cultural,
9 ethnic, linguistic, and religious vibrance of its residents,
10 including Hindu Americans, and
11 WHEREAS, 2026 marks the 133rd anniversary of the date
12 Hinduism was officially introduced to the United States by Swami
13 Vivekananda at the 1893 World's Parliament of Religions in
14 Chicago, Illinois, and the 126th anniversary of the date he
15 founded the Vedanta Society in San Francisco, California, and
16 WHEREAS, Hindus are primarily an immigrant community and
17 first started immigrating to the United States in the early
18 1900s, and
19 WHEREAS, Florida is home to many Hindu temples, religious
20 centers, and cultural centers, and
21 WHEREAS, Hindu Americans are a highly educated demographic,
22 with a 77 percent college attainment rate, and
23 WHEREAS, Hindu organizations and individuals actively
24 engage in seva, a Sanskrit word for selfless service, toward
