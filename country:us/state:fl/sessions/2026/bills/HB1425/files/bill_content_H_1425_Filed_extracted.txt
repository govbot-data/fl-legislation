1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the practice of pharmacy; amending
3 s. 465.0125, F.S.; revising the definition of "health
4 care facility"; authorizing consultant pharmacists to
5 provide medication management services in clinics
6 owned by a hospital or by one or more physicians who
7 are employed by a hospital; amending s. 626.8825,
8 F.S.; defining the term "covered prescription drug";
9 authorizing the administration and dispensing of
10 covered prescription drugs as part of outpatient care;
11 providing an effective date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. Paragraph (e) of subsection (1) of section
16 465.0125, Florida Statutes, is amended to read:
17 465.0125 Consultant pharmacist license; application,
18 renewal, fees; responsibilities; rules.—
19 (1) The department shall issue or renew a consultant
20 pharmacist license upon receipt of an initial or renewal
21 application that conforms to the requirements for consultant
22 pharmacist initial licensure or renewal as adopted by the board
23 by rule and a fee set by the board not to exceed $250. To be
24 licensed as a consultant pharmacist, a pharmacist must complete
25 additional training as required by the board.
