HB 1075 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to municipal utility connections;
3 amending s. 180.19, F.S.; requiring municipalities
4 that operate utility services to allow other
5 municipalities and property owners outside its
6 municipal boundaries to connect with and use its
7 utility services under specified conditions; requiring
8 municipalities to make certain determinations within a
9 specified timeframe; providing for appeal of such
10 determinations; providing for the award of attorney
11 fees and court costs; providing that municipalities
12 are not liable for such fees and costs under certain
13 conditions; providing construction; providing an
14 effective date.
