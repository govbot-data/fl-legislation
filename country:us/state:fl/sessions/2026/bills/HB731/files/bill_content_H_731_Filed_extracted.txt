1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to interscholastic or intrascholastic
3 extracurricular activities; amending s. 1001.43, F.S.;
4 authorizing district school boards to adopt policies
5 for the use of certain funds to provide funding to
6 athletic coaches or activity sponsors of
7 extracurricular activities; prohibiting such funds
8 from being used as severance pay; amending s. 1006.15,
9 F.S.; revising an exemption from a prohibition on
10 certain transfer students participating in specified
11 sports; providing governing association requirements
12 for determinations of eligibility for student
13 participation in certain extracurricular activities;
14 requiring governing associations to adopt specified
15 bylaws; providing applicability; amending s. 1012.01,
16 F.S.; including athletic administrators in the broad
17 classification of K-12 administrative personnel;
18 authorizing certain athletic administrators to
19 negotiate specified compensation; providing
20 requirements for such compensation; providing an
21 effective date.
