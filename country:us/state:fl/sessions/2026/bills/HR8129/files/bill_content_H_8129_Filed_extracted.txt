HR 8129 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution recognizing the 100th anniversary of
3 Black History Month and honoring the achievements,
4 history, and heritage of African Americans in Florida
5 and across the United States.
6
7 WHEREAS, in 1926, Dr. Carter G. Woodson and the Association
8 for the Study of Negro Life and History established "Negro
9 History Week," and
10 WHEREAS, the observance strategically coincided with the
11 February birthdays of Frederick Douglass, a formerly enslaved
12 abolitionist, writer, and statesman, and Abraham Lincoln, the
13 President who issued the Emancipation Proclamation, and
14 WHEREAS, "Negro History Week" grew substantially over the
15 decades, expanding into a monthlong celebration in schools
16 across the country, and
17 WHEREAS, in 1976, President Gerald R. Ford formally
18 recognized February as Black History Month, calling upon
19 citizens to honor the often-neglected accomplishments of Black
20 Americans in every field of human endeavor, and
21 WHEREAS, Florida's rich history includes numerous African
22 American leaders whose accomplishments have shaped the state,
23 including former Florida State Senator Geraldine F. Thompson,
24 who championed historic preservation and African American
25 History and Culture; Dr. Mary McLeod Bethune, whose visionary
