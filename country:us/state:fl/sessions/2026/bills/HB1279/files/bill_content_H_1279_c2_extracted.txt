101 Section 1. Section 413.0114, Florida Statutes, is created
102 to read:
103 413.0114 Consumer disclosure for blind-related services.—
104 (1) Any person, business, nonprofit, or other entity
105 offering fee-based services to persons who are blind or visually
106 impaired must, before entering into a contract or accepting
107 payment, disclose in writing if equivalent or substantially
108 similar services may be available at no cost through the
109 Division of Blind Services or another public agency.
110 (2) The written disclosure must:
111 (a) Be provided in plain language and an accessible
112 format, including, but not limited to, braille, large print, or
113 audio upon request.
114 (b) Include contact information for the Division of Blind
115 Services.
116 (c) Be signed or electronically acknowledged by the
117 consumer or his or her representative.
118 (3) A violation of this section constitutes an unfair or
119 deceptive trade practice under part II of chapter 501, and is
120 subject to penalties and enforcement as provided therein.
121 (4) The State Board of Education may adopt rules to
122 implement this section.
