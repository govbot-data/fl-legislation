HB 665 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to sales tax exemption for motor
3 vehicles sold to veterans; amending s. 212.08, F.S.;
4 creating an exemption from sales and use tax for new
5 motor vehicles purchased by certain veterans of the
6 armed forces; requiring persons seeking such exemption
7 to complete certain forms and provide certain
8 documentation; requiring the Department of Revenue to
9 adopt rules; providing an effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Paragraph (ffff) is added to subsection (7) of
14 section 212.08, Florida Statutes, to read:
15 212.08 Sales, rental, use, consumption, distribution, and
16 storage tax; specified exemptions.—The sale at retail, the
17 rental, the use, the consumption, the distribution, and the
18 storage to be used or consumed in this state of the following
19 are hereby specifically exempt from the tax imposed by this
20 chapter.
21 (7) MISCELLANEOUS EXEMPTIONS.—Exemptions provided to any
22 entity by this chapter do not inure to any transaction that is

24 representative or employee of the entity by any means,

39 (ffff)1. New motor vehicles.—New motor vehicles purchased
40 by a veteran of the armed forces with a service-connected
41 disability rating of 100 percent are exempt from the tax imposed
42 under this chapter if such veteran is a resident of this state.
43 2. To be eligible for an exemption under this paragraph,
44 the person seeking the exemption must complete a form prescribed
45 by the department and submit supporting documentation as
46 required by the department.
