54 Section 1. Section 403.541, Florida Statutes, is created
55 to read:
56 403.541 Advanced nuclear reactors.-
57 (1) Based on the energy needs of this state, economic
58 considerations, statewide energy reliability goals, and the need
59 for additional baseload electrical generating capacity, the
60 Legislature intends to promote the development of nuclear energy
61 and to protect the viability of existing nuclear energy
62 facilities in this state, encourage investment into nuclear
63 energy within this state, and minimize rising electricity rates
64 through the use of nuclear energy.
65 (2) As used in this section, the term:
66 (a) "Advanced nuclear reactor" means a nuclear fusion
67 reactor, or a nuclear fission reactor that uses new or
68 significantly improved designs compared to commercial nuclear
69 reactors in operation as of January 1, 2021, including
70 improvements such as:
71 1. Inherent or passive safety features;
72 2. Lower waste yields;
73 3. Improved fuel performance or efficiency;
74 4. Significantly improved designs, materials, fuels, or
75 cooling systems;
