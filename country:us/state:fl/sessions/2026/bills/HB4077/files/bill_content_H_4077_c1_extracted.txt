CS/HB 4077 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the City of Punta Gorda, Charlotte
3 County; providing a short title; providing legislative
4 intent; providing boundaries of the Punta Gorda
5 Utility District; authorizing Charlotte County to
6 assume responsibility for utility functions in lands
7 outside the district; authorizing the City of Punta
8 Gorda to continue to own and maintain assets outside
9 the district; requiring the city and the county to
10 enter into interlocal agreements for specified
11 purposes; providing reporting requirements; requiring
12 the city to continue to provide potable water and
13 wastewater utility service to certain customers and to
14 maintain the potable water and wastewater utility
15 service infrastructure under certain circumstances;
16 providing construction; requiring continuation of
17 powers, duties, and obligations of the district and
18 city until the transfer; providing an effective date.
19
20 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
21
22 Section 1. Short title.—This act may be cited as the
23 "Punta Gorda Utility District Boundary Codification and Utility
24 Transfer Act."

26 (1) The boundaries of the Punta Gorda Utility District
27 must be established.
28 (2) The district is wholly located within the corporate
29 boundaries of Charlotte County, which provides potable water and
30 wastewater utility services.
31 (3) The public interest is best served by authorizing the
32 county to provide potable water and wastewater utility services
33 for certain uncertificated lands lying outside the district,
34 which will ensure uninterrupted service and efficient regional
35 utility management.
36 Section 3. Establishment of district boundaries.—
37 (1) The boundaries of the Punta Gorda Utility District are
38 hereby codified to include all lands lying within the corporate
39 limits of the City of Punta Gorda, Charlotte County, as such
40 municipal boundaries exist on the effective date of this act.
41 (2) The City of Punta Gorda shall provide the Department
42 of State and the Department of Commerce a certified map and
43 metes and bounds legal description of the city's municipal
44 boundaries as of the effective date of this act. This map and
45 description are incorporated by reference and constitute the
46 official boundaries of the district.

48 utility services.—
