1 House Joint Resolution
2 A joint resolution proposing an amendment to Section 4
3 of Article VII and the creation of a new section in
4 Article XII of the State Constitution to authorize the
5 Legislature to provide, by general law, that the
6 transfer of homestead property by inheritance is not
7 considered to be a change in ownership for purposes of
8 homestead property assessment and provide an effective
9 date.

15 Constitution is agreed to and shall be submitted to the electors
16 of this state for approval or rejection at the next general
17 election or at an earlier special election specifically
18 authorized by law for that purpose:
