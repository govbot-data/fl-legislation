1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Task Force on Artificial
3 Intelligence in Public Postsecondary Education;
4 creating the Task Force on Artificial Intelligence in
5 Public Postsecondary Education; requiring the task
6 force to convene by a specified date; providing the
7 purpose of the task force; providing for the
8 membership of the task force; providing duties of the
9 task force; requiring the task force to provide a
10 report to the Governor and Legislature by a specified
11 date; providing for the future termination of the task
12 force; providing an effective date.
13
14 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
15
16 Section 1. (1) The Task Force on Artificial Intelligence
17 in Public Postsecondary Education, a task force as defined in s.
18 20.03(5), Florida Statutes, is created adjunct to the Department
19 of Education. The task force shall convene no later than August
20 1, 2026, to review and make recommendations on the
21 opportunities, risks, and best practices presented by artificial
22 intelligence in academic instruction, research, and student
23 conduct within the State University System and Florida College
24 System.
25 (2) The task force shall consist of the following members:
