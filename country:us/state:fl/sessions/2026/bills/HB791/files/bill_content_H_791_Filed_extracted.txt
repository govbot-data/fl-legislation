214 Section 4. Section 212.056, Florida Statutes, is created
215 to read:
216 212.056 Property Sales Surtax.—
217 (1) On any transaction required to pay the tax under s.
218 201.02 or s. 201.133, there shall be a sales tax of 5 percent on
219 the consideration.
220 (2) This tax shall be collected in the same manner as the
221 taxes under ss. 201.02 and 201.133.
222 (3) The Department of Revenue shall pay to the governing
223 authority of each county all surtaxes collected pursuant to this
224 section related to property located in such county.
