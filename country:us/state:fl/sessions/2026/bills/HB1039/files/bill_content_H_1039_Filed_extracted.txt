42 Section 1. Section 17.613, Florida Statutes, is created to

44 17.613 State cryptocurrency reserve.—
45 (1) DEFINITIONS.—As used in this section, the term:
46 (a) "Airdrop" means a gratuitous distribution of
47 cryptocurrency to users of a blockchain or protocol, generally
48 made in a broad, equitable, and nondiscretionary manner.
49 (b) "Committee" means the Florida Strategic Cryptocurrency
50 Reserve Advisory Committee established in this section.

102 (2) ESTABLISHMENT; ADMINISTRATION.—
103 (a) The Florida Strategic Cryptocurrency Reserve is
104 established as a special fund outside the State Treasury. The
105 Chief Financial Officer has custody of and shall administer and
106 manage the reserve. The reserve consists of:
107 1. Money transferred or deposited to the credit of the
108 reserve by legislative appropriation.
109 2. Revenue that the Legislature by general law dedicates
110 for deposit to the credit of the reserve.
111 3. Cryptocurrency purchased using money in or received by
112 the reserve, including any cryptocurrency:
113 a. Derived from the fork of a distributed ledger; or
114 b. Distributed pursuant to an airdrop to this state's
115 cryptocurrency addresses.
116 4. Investment earnings and interest or rewards earned on
117 assets in the reserve.
118 (b) To administer and manage the reserve, the Chief
119 Financial Officer may acquire, exchange, sell, supervise,
120 manage, or retain any kind of investments that a prudent
121 investor exercising reasonable care, skill, and caution would
122 acquire, exchange, sell, supervise, manage, or retain in light
123 of the purposes, terms, distribution requirements, and other
124 circumstances then prevailing for the reserve, taking into
125 consideration the investment of all the assets of the reserve
