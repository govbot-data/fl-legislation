F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 1339 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to required coverage for imaging and
3 genetic testing for cancer risk; creating ss.
4 627.64192, 627.66912, and 641.31078, F.S.; providing
5 definitions; requiring certain individual health
6 policies, group, blanket, and franchise policies, and
7 health maintenance contracts, respectively, to provide
8 coverage for evidence-based cancer imaging and genetic
9 testing for hereditary cancer risk, without any cost-
10 sharing requirements, for specified individuals;
11 providing an effective date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. Section 627.64192, Florida Statutes, is created
16 to read:
17 627.64192 Required coverage for imaging and genetic
18 testing for cancer risk.—
19 (1) As used in this section, the term:
20 (a) "Cost-sharing requirement" means an insured's
21 deductible, coinsurance, copayment, or any similar out-of-pocket
22 expense.
23 (b) "Evidence-based cancer imaging" means imaging
24 techniques used for detecting cancer and guiding treatment which
25 are in accordance with the most recent versions of the National
