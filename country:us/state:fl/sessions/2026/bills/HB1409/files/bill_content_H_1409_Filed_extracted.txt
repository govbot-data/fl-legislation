168 commits a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as
169 provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083. A second or subsequent
170 violation of this subparagraph less than 1 year after the first
171 violation is a misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as
172 provided in s. 775.082 or s. 775.083.

226 (5) THC-INFUSED BEVERAGE RETAIL VENDOR.—A retail vendor of
