F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
CS/HB 1483 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to trust funds; creating s. 420.56,
3 F.S.; creating the Vacancy Relief and Risk Mitigation
4 Trust Fund within the Florida Housing Finance
5 Corporation for a specified purpose; authorizing any
6 unexpended balance at a specified time to revert and
7 be reappropriated for a certain purpose; providing for
8 future review and termination or re-creation of the
9 trust fund; providing a contingent effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Section 420.56, Florida Statutes, is created to
14 read:
15 420.56 Homes for Veterans Property Management Incentive
16 Pilot Program trust fund.—
17 (1) The Vacancy Relief and Risk Mitigation Trust Fund is
18 created within the Florida Housing Finance Corporation, to which
19 the Legislature may appropriate funds for the purpose of
20 implementing the Homes for Veterans Property Management
21 Incentive Pilot Program under s. 83.684.
22 (2) Notwithstanding s. 216.301 and pursuant to s. 216.351,
23 any balance in the trust fund at the end of the fiscal year
24 shall revert and be appropriated to the corporation for the next
25 fiscal year for carrying out the purposes of the trust fund.
