CS/HB 999 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to refrigerant safety and agricultural
3 continuity; creating s. 604.74, F.S.; providing a
4 short title; providing legislative findings; defining
5 the terms "A2L refrigerant" and "department";
6 requiring the Department of Agriculture and Consumer
7 Services to provide an annual report to the
8 Legislature by a specified date; providing
9 requirements for the annual report; authorizing the
10 department to coordinate with certain entities for a
11 specified purpose; providing an effective date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. Section 604.74, Florida Statutes, is created to
16 read:
17 604.74 Refrigerant safety and agricultural continuity.—
18 (1) This section may be cited as the "Refrigerant Safety
19 and Agricultural Continuity Act."
20 (2) The Legislature finds that this state's agricultural
21 food processing and cold chain industries rely upon refrigerant
22 systems that ensure product safety, temperature stability, and
23 export reliability. The United States Environmental Protection
24 Agency's Technology Transition Rule requires replacement of R-
25 410A refrigerants with A2L refrigerants, including R-454B
