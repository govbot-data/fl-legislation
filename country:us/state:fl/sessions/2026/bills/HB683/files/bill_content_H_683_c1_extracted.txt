1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the performance of physician

7 prescribing authority to a physician assistant;

12 amending s. 464.012, F.S.; revising the drug formulary
13 requirements for advanced practice registered nurses
14 authorized to prescribe medications; providing an
15 exception for psychiatric nurses; providing an
16 effective date.
