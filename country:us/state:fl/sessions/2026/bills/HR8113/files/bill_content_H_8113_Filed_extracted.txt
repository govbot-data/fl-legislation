HR 8113 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating March 2026 as "Developmental
3 Disabilities Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, developmental disabilities are a group of
6 conditions that cause impairments in physical, learning,
7 language, or behavioral areas, often beginning during the
8 developmental period and lasting throughout a person's lifetime,
9 and
10 WHEREAS, developmental disabilities include conditions such
11 as autism spectrum disorders, cerebral palsy, attention-deficit
12 hyperactivity disorder, learning or intellectual disabilities,
13 hearing loss, vision impairment, and other developmental delays,
14 and
15 WHEREAS, approximately one in six children in the United
16 States are diagnosed with a developmental disability, and
17 WHEREAS, thousands of individuals with developmental
18 disabilities in Florida contribute to their communities,
19 workplaces, and schools when provided equitable access to
20 healthcare, education, and employment opportunities, and
21 WHEREAS, "Developmental Disabilities Awareness Month" was
22 established in 1987 by President Ronald Reagan to highlight the
23 importance of fostering a society where people with disabilities
24 are empowered to contribute to their communities, and
