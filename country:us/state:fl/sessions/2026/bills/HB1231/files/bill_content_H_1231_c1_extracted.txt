61 497.005 Definitions.—As used in this chapter, the term:
62 (22) "Cremation" means any mechanical or thermal process
63 whereby a dead human body is reduced to ashes and bone
64 fragments. Cremation also includes any other mechanical or
65 thermal process whereby human remains are pulverized, burned,
66 recremated, or otherwise further reduced in size or quantity,
67 including natural organic reduction.
68 (50) "Natural organic reduction" means the contained,
69 accelerated conversion of human remains to soil.
70 (51) "Natural organic reduction facility" means a
71 structure, room, or other space in a building or a real property
72 where natural organic reduction of a human body occurs.
