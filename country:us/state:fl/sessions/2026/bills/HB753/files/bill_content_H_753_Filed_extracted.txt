F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 753 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to certified school counselors;
3 amending s. 1012.55, F.S.; providing that persons
4 employed as certified school counselors are exempt
5 from specified educator certification requirements,
6 unless required for employment by a school district;
7 providing an effective date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11 Section 1. Subsection (6) of section 1012.55, Florida
12 Statutes, is renumbered as subsection (7), and a new subsection
13 (6) is added to that section, to read:
14 1012.55 Positions for which certificates required.—
15 (6) A person who is employed as a certified school
16 counselor is exempt from the educator certification requirements
17 in s. 1012.56(2)(g) and (h) relating to the mastery of general
18 and subject area knowledge, unless required by a school district
19 for employment.
20 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
