F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 941 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to trust funds; re-creating the
3 Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund within the
4 Executive Office of the Governor; amending s.
5 252.3711, F.S.; abrogating provisions relating to the
6 termination of the trust fund; providing an effective
7 date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11 Section 1. The Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund
12 within the Executive Office of the Governor, which is to be
13 terminated pursuant to Section 19(f), Article III of the State
14 Constitution on February 17, 2026, is re-created.
15 Section 2. Subsection (3) of section 252.3711, Florida
16 Statutes, is amended to read:
17 252.3711 Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund.—
18 (3) In accordance with s. 19(f)(2), Art. III of the State
19 Constitution, the Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund
20 shall, unless terminated sooner, be terminated 4 years after the
21 effective date of this act. Before its scheduled termination,
22 the fund shall be reviewed as provided in s. 215.3206(1) and
23 (2).
24 Section 3. This act shall take effect upon becoming a law.
