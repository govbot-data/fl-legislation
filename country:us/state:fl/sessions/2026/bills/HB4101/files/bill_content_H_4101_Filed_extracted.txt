1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Town of Loxahatchee Groves,
3 Palm Beach County; providing an exception to general
4 law; prohibiting the sale and use of fireworks within
5 the Town of Loxahatchee Groves agricultural
6 residential zoning district except under specified
7 circumstances; providing applicability; providing an
8 effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. Notwithstanding ss. 791.001 and 791.08, Florida
13 Statutes, the sale and use of fireworks within the Town of
14 Loxahatchee Groves agricultural residential (AR) zoning district
15 is prohibited on all days of the year except for the following:
16 (1) Supervised public displays within the jurisdictional
17 boundaries of the Town of Loxahatchee Groves where such displays
18 are handled by a competent operator to be approved by the Palm
19 Beach County Sheriff's Office and Fire Department, and shall be
20 of such a character, and so located, discharged, or fired as in
21 the opinion of the chief of the fire department, after proper
22 inspection, shall not be hazardous to property or endanger any
23 person. Application for permits shall be made in writing at
24 least 15 days in advance of the date of the display. After such
25 privilege shall have been granted, sales, possession, use, and
