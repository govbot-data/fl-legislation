1 House Resolution
2 A resolution recognizing February 14, 2026, as
3 "Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School Memorial Day" in
4 Florida, a day of remembrance for all of those who
5 lost their lives, or whose lives were forever changed,
6 as a result of the mass shooting at Marjory Stoneman
7 Douglas High School in Parkland.
8
9 WHEREAS, on February 14, 2018, Valentine's Day, students,
10 faculty, and staff at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School in
11 Parkland arrived on campus for a school day that began like any
12 other, and
13 WHEREAS, at 2:19 p.m., a 19-year-old former student of the
14 school was dropped off by an Uber driver on the east side of the
15 Marjory Stoneman Douglas campus, and
16 WHEREAS, the young man was carrying a rifle bag, and
17 minutes later entered the east hallway doors of Building 12 on
18 the campus, where he removed his semiautomatic rifle from the
19 bag, loaded the weapon, and donned a magazine-carrying vest, and
20 WHEREAS, at 2:21 p.m., the young man fired the first rounds
21 to the west of the first floor hallway, striking four students,
22 only one of whom survived her injuries, and
23 WHEREAS, in the minutes that followed, the young man
24 repeatedly fired into classrooms and at those within his line of
25 sight in the hallway, and

100 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of
101 Florida:
102
103 That we remember the tragic events of February 14, 2018,
104 and all of the lives lost or forever changed due to the mass
105 shooting at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School.
106 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that February 14, 2026, is
107 recognized as "Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School Memorial
108 Day" in Florida.
109 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that a copy of this resolution be
110 available for presentation to the surviving family members of
111 those who lost their lives in the mass shooting at Marjory
112 Stoneman Douglas High School as a tangible token of the
113 sentiments expressed herein.
