15
16 Section 1. Section 267.23, Florida Statutes, is created to
17 read:
18 267.23 Historic Cemeteries Trust Fund.—
19 (1) The Historic Cemeteries Trust Fund is created within
20 the division. The purpose of the trust fund is to act as a
21 depository for the long-term maintenance and upkeep of
22 cemeteries under the Historic Cemeteries Program.
23 (2) Moneys must be deposited into the trust fund and
24 disbursed by the division as provided in s. 267.21.
