HR 8011 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating the week of February 23-March
3 1, 2026, as "Eating Disorders Awareness Week" in
4 Florida.
5
6 WHEREAS, eating disorders affect 28.8 million Americans,
7 including 1.8 million Floridians, without regard to gender,
8 gender identity, age, race, ethnicity, body shape and weight,
9 ability, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status, and
10 WHEREAS, eating disorders, specifically anorexia nervosa,
11 bulimia nervosa, binge eating disorder, avoidant restrictive
12 food intake disorder, and other feeding or eating disorders, are
13 complex, biologically based illnesses, and
14 WHEREAS, eating disorders are highly prevalent among
15 servicemembers and veterans of the United States Armed Forces,
16 and
17 WHEREAS, eating disorders are associated with serious
18 physical health consequences, including irregular heartbeat,
19 heart disease and heart failure, kidney failure, osteoporosis,
20 gastric rupture, tooth decay, obesity, gallbladder disease,
21 diabetes, and death, and
22 WHEREAS, in the United States, 10,200 deaths occur annually
23 as a direct result of an eating disorder, equating to one death
24 every 52 minutes, and
