HB 735 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Division of State Fire Marshal;
3 amending s. 633.408, F.S.; requiring the Division of
4 State Fire Marshal to accept training and
5 certification of firefighters from another
6 jurisdiction under certain circumstances; requiring
7 the division to make a specified finding before
8 denying a firefighter certificate; defining the term
9 "basis certificate"; amending ss. 633.508 and 633.520,
10 F.S.; requiring the division to adopt certain rules
11 relating to contaminated personal protective equipment
12 and firefighting gear; providing an effective date.
13
14 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
15
16 Section 1. Subsection (2) of section 633.408, Florida
17 Statutes, is amended, and subsection (10) is added to that
18 section, to read:
19 633.408 Firefighter and volunteer firefighter training and
20 certification.—
21 (2) Except as provided in subsection (10), courses under
22 subsection (1) may only be administered by education or training
23 providers approved by the division pursuant to s. 633.128(1)(c)
24 and taught by instructors certified pursuant to s.
25 633.128(1)(d).
