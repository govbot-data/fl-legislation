143 905.275 Preservation of grand jury materials.—
144 (1) The clerk of court shall take possession of and
145 preserve all materials presented or created during a grand jury
146 proceeding. A person may not dispose of or destroy such
147 materials.
148 (2) For purposes of this section, the term "materials"
149 includes all of the following:
150 (a) Digital recordings.
