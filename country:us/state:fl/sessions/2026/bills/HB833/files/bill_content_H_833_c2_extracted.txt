1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to private school facilities; amending
3 s. 1002.42, F.S.; providing that certain private
4 schools are considered a permitted use in certain
5 zoning districts; authorizing certain private schools
6 to operate in facilities that meet specified
7 requirements; requiring certain private schools
8 operating in such facilities to meet specified Florida
9 Fire Prevention Code standards; providing that
10 completion of a specified evaluation system with
11 certain ratings by specified persons constitutes
12 evidence of compliance with the Florida Fire
13 Prevention Code for such private schools; authorizing
14 the State Fire Marshal to adopt rules; providing an
15 effective date.
