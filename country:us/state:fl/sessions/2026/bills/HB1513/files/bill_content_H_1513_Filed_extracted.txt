9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

11 Section 1. Subsection (136) is added to section 320.08058,
12 Florida Statutes, to read:
13 320.08058 Specialty license plates.—
14 (136) ITALIAN-AMERICAN CULTURE LICENSE PLATES.—
15 (a) The department shall develop an Italian-American

17 320.08053. The plate must bear the colors and design approved by
18 the department. The word "Florida" must appear at the top of the
19 plate, and the words "Italian Heritage" must appear at the
20 bottom of the plate.

22 distributed to Il Circola, Inc., a nonprofit corporation under
23 s. 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, which may use up to
24 10 percent of the fees for administrative costs and marketing of
25 the plate. Il Circola, Inc., must use the remainder of the fees
