28 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

30 Section 1. Section 1009.8962, Florida Statutes, is amended
31 to read:
32 1009.8962 Linking Industry to Nursing Education (LINE)
33 Fund.—
34 (1) This section shall be known and may be cited as the
35 "Linking Industry to Nursing Education (LINE) Fund Act."
36 (2) Recognizing that the state has a persistent and
37 growing nursing shortage, it is the intent of the Legislature to
38 address this critical workforce need by incentivizing
39 collaboration between nursing education programs and health care
40 partners that contribute monetary or nonmonetary contributions
41 through the establishment of the LINE Fund. This fund is
42 intended to meet local, regional, and state workforce demand by
43 recruiting faculty and clinical preceptors, increasing the
44 capacity of high-quality nursing education programs, and
45 increasing the number of nursing education program graduates who
46 are prepared and licensed to enter the workforce.
47 (3) As used in this section, the term:
48 (a) "Health care partner" means a health care provider as
49 defined in s. 768.38(2) which is licensed to operate in this
50 state.
