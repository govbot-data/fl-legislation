26 (1) This section shall be known and may be cited as the
27 "Linking Industry to Nursing Education (LINE) Fund Act."
28 (2) Recognizing that the state has a persistent and
29 growing nursing shortage, it is the intent of the Legislature to
30 address this critical workforce need by incentivizing
31 collaboration among between nursing education programs, and
32 health care partners, and other persons and entities that
33 contribute monetary or nonmonetary contributions through the
34 establishment of the LINE Fund. This fund is intended to meet
35 local, regional, and state workforce demand by recruiting
36 faculty and clinical preceptors, increasing the capacity of
37 high-quality nursing education programs, and increasing the
38 number of nursing education program graduates who are prepared
39 to enter the workforce.
40 (3) As used in this section, the term:
41 (a) "Health care partner" means a health care provider as
42 defined in s. 768.38(2).
43 (b) "Institution" means a school district career center
44 under s. 1001.44; a charter technical career center under s.
45 1002.34; a Florida College System institution; a state
46 university; an independent nonprofit college or university
47 located and chartered in this state and accredited by an agency
48 or association that is recognized by the database created and
49 maintained by the United States Department of Education to grant
50 baccalaureate degrees; or an independent school, college, or
