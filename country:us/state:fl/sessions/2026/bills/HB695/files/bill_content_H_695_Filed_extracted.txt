1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to public records and meetings;
3 creating ss. 401.4661 and 456.661, F.S.; providing
4 exemptions from public meetings requirements for
5 certain portions of meetings of the Interstate
6 Commission for EMS Personnel Practice and the
7 Physician Assistant Licensure Compact Commission;
8 respectively; providing an exemption from public
9 records requirements for recordings, minutes, and
10 records generated during exempt portions of such
11 meetings; providing for future legislative review and
12 repeal of the exemptions; providing a statement of
13 public necessity; providing a contingent effective
14 date.
