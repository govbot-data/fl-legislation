138 Section 4. Section 163.212, Florida Statutes, is created

140 163.212 Prohibiting local government expenditure of public
141 funds for diversity, equity, and inclusion.—
142 (1) A local government may not expend public funds, or
143 otherwise expend any funds derived from bequests, charges,
144 deposits, donations, grants, gifts, income, receipts, or any
145 other source of funds, for the purpose of diversity, equity, and
146 inclusion.
147 (2)(a) A local government may not contract with a private
148 vendor for the provision of services for promoting, advocating
149 for, or providing training or education on diversity, equity,
150 and inclusion.
