2 An act relating to the Coconut Grove Playhouse, Miami-

63 public use, and cultural benefit.

65 the Coconut Grove Playhouse, the Town of Bay Harbor Islands
66 shall consult with the City of Miami, the Department of
67 Environmental Protection, and the Division of Historical

69 alternatives to the demolition or alteration of the playhouse,
70 as required under s. 267.061(2)(b), Florida Statutes, and on
71 every renovation it plans to perform to protect the historic and
72 cultural value of the property.
