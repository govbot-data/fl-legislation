54 Section 1. Section 50.0311, Florida Statutes, is amended
55 to read:
56 50.0311 Publication of advertisements and public notices
57 on a publicly accessible website and governmental access
58 channels.—
59 (1) For purposes of this chapter, the term "governmental
60 agency" means a county, municipality, school board, clerk of the
61 circuit court, tax collector, water management district
62 operating pursuant to chapter 373, or other unit of local
63 government or political subdivision in this state.
64 (2) For purposes of notices and advertisements required
65 under s. 50.011, the term "publicly accessible website" means a
66 county's official website or other private website designated by
67 the county for the publication of legal notices and
68 advertisements which that is accessible through via the
69 Internet. For legal notices and advertisements required under s.
70 50.011 by a municipality, clerk of the circuit court, tax
71 collector, water management district, or other unit of local
72 government, the term "publicly accessible website" means such
73 entity's official website, a private website designated by such
74 entity, a county's official website, or a private website
75 designated by the county in which such entity is located.
