F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 1361 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to public records requests pertaining
3 to law enforcement officers; amending s. 119.07, F.S.;
4 requiring certain identification from a person
5 requesting to inspect or copy a public record relating
6 to a law enforcement officer; requiring the custodian
7 of public records to retain a copy of such
8 identification; providing applicability; providing an
9 effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Paragraph (e) is added to subsection (3) of
14 section 119.07, Florida Statutes, to read:
15 119.07 Inspection and copying of records; photographing
16 public records; identification required; fees; exemptions.—
17 (3)
18 (e) Any person requesting to inspect or copy a public
19 record relating to a law enforcement officer as defined in s.
20 943.10(1) must provide the custodian of public records with a
21 valid driver license or identification card issued by this state
22 or another state. The custodian of public records must retain a
23 copy of such license or identification card with such request.
24 This paragraph does not apply to a law enforcement officer
25 acting in his or her official capacity.
