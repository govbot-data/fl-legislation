112 to read:
113 163.3203 Local land planning and development program.—
114 (1)(a) The Legislature recognizes the continued need for
115 local land planning and development strategies to achieve growth
116 and land development throughout this state.
117 (b) The Legislature recognizes the need to balance the
118 role of local governments in community planning and development.
119 (c) The Legislature recognizes the benefits of local
120 government implementation of innovative planning and development
121 strategies through the use of the private sector to supplement
122 the needs of local government and to keep pace with population
123 growth, unmet demands for housing, and continuing budget
124 constraints.
