54 Section 2. Section 943.0421, Florida Statutes, is created
55 to read:
56 943.0421 Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force
57 Funding Program.—
58 (1) SHORT TITLE.—This section may be cited as the "Florida
59 Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force Funding Program
60 Act."
61 (2) LEGISLATIVE FINDINGS.—The Legislature finds that:
62 (a) The Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force
63 program, run through the United States Department of Justice,
64 helps state and local law enforcement agencies develop an
65 effective response to technology-facilitated child sexual
66 exploitation and Internet crimes against children. This help
67 encompasses forensic and investigative components, training and
68 technical assistance, victim services, and community education.
69 The program is a national network of 61 coordinated task forces
70 representing over 5,400 federal, state, and local law
71 enforcement and prosecutorial agencies.
72 (b) There is a lack of dedicated state resources to combat
73 Internet crimes against children. As a result, many of the cases
74 involving Internet crimes are not adequately investigated. The
75 Legislature intends to create an account dedicated to combating

79 (3) DEFINITION.—The term "task force" means an Internet
