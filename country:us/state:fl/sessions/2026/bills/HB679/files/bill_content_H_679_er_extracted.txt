201 495.0315 Online application for registration.—
202 (1) The department shall establish and maintain a secure
203 Internet website that allows an applicant to submit an online
204 trademark registration application in accordance with ss.
205 495.031 and 495.035 and a renewal application in accordance with
206 s. 495.071. The website must safeguard the applicant's
207 information to ensure data integrity and allow the applicant to:
208 (a) Provide an electronic version of a required specimen
209 of use.
210 (b) Provide an electronic version of a drawing of the
211 mark.
212 (c) Pay the fee required by s. 495.191.
213 (d) Complete the verification required by s. 495.031(5).
214 (2) The department shall make the online application
215 system required by this section available no later than December
216 31, 2027.
217 Section 3. Subsections (5) and (6) of section 495.031,
218 Florida Statutes, are amended to read:
219 495.031 Application for registration.—
220 (5) Every application under this section shall be signed
221 and verified by the applicant or by a member of the firm or an
222 officer or other authorized representative of the business
223 entity applying. Verification must be made in accordance with s.
224 92.525 and may be accomplished electronically in compliance with
225 ss. 668.001-668.006.
