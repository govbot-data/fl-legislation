2 An act relating to homestead tax exemptions; amending

14 Section 1. Paragraph (a) of subsection (4) of section
15 193.155, Florida Statutes, is amended to read:
16 193.155 Homestead assessments.—Homestead property shall be
17 assessed at just value as of January 1, 1994. Property receiving
18 the homestead exemption after January 1, 1994, shall be assessed

20 receives the exemption unless the provisions of subsection (8)
21 apply.
22 (4)(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) and s. 193.624,
23 changes, additions, or improvements to homestead property shall
24 be assessed at just value as of the first January 1 after the
