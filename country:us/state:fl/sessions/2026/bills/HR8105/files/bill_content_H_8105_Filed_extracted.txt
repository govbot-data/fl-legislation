1 House Resolution
2 A resolution recognizing the life and legacy of E.
3 Lilyan Spencer on the 120th anniversary of her birth.
4
5 WHEREAS, E. Lilyan Spencer was born in 1906, in
6 Tallahassee, Florida, to the Rev. Abraham B. Spencer (1863-
7 1924), of Decatur, Georgia and Lucinda C. Stroman Spencer (1871-
8 1933), of Tallahassee, and
9 WHEREAS, E. Lilyan Spencer was a lifelong Tallahassee
10 native, graduating from the original Lincoln High School and
11 earning a bachelor's degree in mathematics with a special
12 certification in administration and supervision from Florida
13 Agricultural and Mechanical University (FAMU), and
14 WHEREAS, throughout her life, E. Lilyan Spencer was a
15 distinguished leader in athletics, education, business, and
16 community service, and
17 WHEREAS, playing professional tennis throughout the 1930s,
18 E. Lilyan Spencer was a nationally celebrated athlete, playing
19 on the segregated national tennis circuit and winning 10 state
20 championships, and
21 WHEREAS, in 1931, E. Lilyan Spencer was the Florida Tennis
22 Association Women's Singles Champion, and she held this title
23 for 10 years without suffering a defeat in competition, and
24 WHEREAS, E. Lilyan Spencer secured her place in
25 professional tennis history by winning a national tennis title
