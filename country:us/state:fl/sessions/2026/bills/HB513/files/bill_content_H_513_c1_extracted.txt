1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Alzheimer's Disease Awareness
3 Initiative; creating s. 430.5016, F.S.; requiring the
4 Department of Elderly Affairs to contract for the
5 development and implementation of the Alzheimer's
6 Disease Awareness Initiative; providing requirements
7 for the initiative; providing requirements for the
8 type of entity with which the department contracts;
9 requiring the Alzheimer's Disease Advisory Committee
10 to make certain recommendations to the department and
11 the Legislature annually; providing an effective date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. Section 430.5016, Florida Statutes, is created
16 to read:
17 430.5016 Alzheimer's Disease Awareness Initiative.—
18 (1) The Department of Elderly Affairs shall contract for
19 the development and implementation of the Alzheimer's Disease
20 Awareness Initiative. The initiative must provide an opportunity
21 for residents of this state affected by Alzheimer's disease and
22 related forms of dementia to obtain information that is
23 validated by national research.
24 (2) The initiative must, at a minimum:
