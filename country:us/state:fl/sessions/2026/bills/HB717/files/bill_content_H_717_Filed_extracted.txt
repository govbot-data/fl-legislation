F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 717 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to autism spectrum disorder; amending
3 s. 393.063, F.S.; revising the definition of the term
4 "autism"; amending s. 1012.582, F.S.; requiring,
5 rather than authorizing, the State Board of Education
6 to adopt certain rules; requiring that such rules
7 include a requirement related to autism spectrum
8 disorder for certain K-12 teachers; providing an
9 effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Subsection (5) of section 393.063, Florida
14 Statutes, is amended to read:
15 393.063 Definitions.—For the purposes of this chapter, the
16 term:
17 (5) "Autism" and "autism spectrum disorder" mean means a
18 condition as defined by the most recent edition of the
19 Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders of the
20 American Psychiatric Association pervasive, neurologically based
21 developmental disability of extended duration which causes
22 severe learning, communication, and behavior disorders with age
23 of onset during infancy or childhood. Individuals with autism
24 exhibit impairment in reciprocal social interaction, impairment
25 in verbal and nonverbal communication and imaginative ability,

28 Section 2. Subsection (4) of section 1012.582, Florida
29 Statutes, is amended to read:
30 1012.582 Continuing education and inservice training for
31 teaching students with developmental and emotional or behavioral
32 disabilities.—
33 (4)(a) The State Board of Education shall may adopt rules
34 pursuant to ss. 120.536(1) and 120.54 to implement this section.
35 (b) Such rules must require a K-12 teacher who teaches a
36 class with at least 50 percent of students having autism
37 spectrum disorder to hold an Autism Spectrum Disorder
38 Endorsement.
39 (c) Such rules must allow for an exception to the Autism
40 Spectrum Disorder Endorsement requirement in paragraph (b) if a
41 teacher holds an Endorsement in Severe or Profound Disabilities,
42 has taught students with autism spectrum disorder for a minimum
43 of 3 years, and has been rated as highly effective in the
44 preceding 3-year period pursuant to s. 1012.34.
45 Section 3. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
