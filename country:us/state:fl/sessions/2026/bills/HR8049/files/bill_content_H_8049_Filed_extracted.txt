1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating July 2026 as "Fibroid
3 Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, uterine fibroids, clinically known as uterine
6 leiomyoma, are benign tumors in the uterus and the single most
7 common indication for hysterectomy, and
8 WHEREAS, uterine fibroids grow on or in the uterus and
9 cause heavy menstrual bleeding, debilitating pain, painful
10 intercourse, fetal mortality, and sometimes infertility in
11 women, and
12 WHEREAS, consequently, uterine fibroids are the leading
13 cause of loss of reproductive potential, and nearly one-half of
14 all hysterectomies are performed to address uterine fibroids,
15 and
16 WHEREAS, as many as three out of four women will have
17 uterine fibroids during their lives, and up to 80 percent of
18 women by age 50 will be affected by uterine fibroids, and
19 WHEREAS, in 2015, the Florida House of Representatives
20 designated July as Fibroid Awareness Month, highlighting the
21 conversation around the disorder, and bringing awareness to the
22 issue, and
23 WHEREAS, public awareness empowers and informs residents of
24 this state about uterine fibroids and encourages earlier
25 diagnosis, more research, and better treatment options, and,
