HB 1025 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the notification of sexual violence
3 policies and supportive measure in K-20 education;
4 creating s. 1000.09, F.S.; providing definitions;
5 requiring public and private K-20 educational
6 institutions to have specified sexual violence
7 policies; authorizing such educational institutions to
8 have specified supportive measures; providing
9 requirements for such policies and measures; requiring
10 schools to notify students, parents, school staff, and
11 school volunteers of such policies and measures;
12 providing requirements for such notifications;
13 providing an effective date.
14
