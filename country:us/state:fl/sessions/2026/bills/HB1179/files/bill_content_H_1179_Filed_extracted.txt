F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 1179 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to public records and meetings;
3 creating s. 331.3261, F.S.; providing an exemption
4 from public records and public meetings requirements
5 for Space Florida; providing for future legislative
6 review and repeal of the exemption; providing a
7 statement of public necessity; providing a contingent
8 effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. Section 331.3261, Florida Statutes, is created
13 to read:
14 331.3261 Information relating to confidential trade
15 secrets.—Any trade secret as defined in s. 812.081(1) held by
16 Space Florida, any spaceport user, or any space industry
17 business is confidential and exempt from s. 119.07(1) and s.
18 24(a), Art. I of the State Constitution. If Space Florida
19 determines that any information requested by the public will
20 reveal a trade secret, Space Florida shall inform, in writing,
21 the person making the request of that determination. The
22 determination is a final order as defined in s. 120.52. Any
23 meeting or portion thereof of the board of directors of Space
24 Florida during which a confidential and exempt trade secret is
25 discussed is exempt from s. 286.011 and s. 24(b), Art. I of the

34 Section 2. The Legislature finds that it is a public
35 necessity that any trade secret as defined in s. 812.081(1),
36 Florida Statutes, held by Space Florida, any spaceport user, and
37 any space industry business be made confidential and exempt from
38 s. 119.07(1), Florida Statutes, and s. 24(a), Article I of the
39 State Constitution. Trade secrets, including, but not limited
40 to, proprietary confidential business information such as secret
41 processes, manufacturing and production methods, and potential
42 and existing trade secrets embodied in pending patent
43 applications, are a valuable asset that enable innovation and
44 provide a competitive advantage in the marketplace. Disclosure
45 of such trade secrets could injure Space Florida, spaceport
46 users, and space industry businesses in the marketplace by
47 revealing proprietary confidential business information to
48 competitors. The space industry is a key component of this
49 state's economy, and the harm that may result from the release
50 of such trade secrets significantly outweighs any public benefit
