1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to computer science education and
3 certification; amending s. 1007.25, F.S.; providing
4 requirements for general education core courses with a
5 technology component; amending s. 1007.2616, F.S.;
6 requiring high school computer science courses to
7 include instruction on artificial intelligence;
8 requiring the State Board of Education to establish
9 separate computer science subject area coverages for
10 specified grades and to continue the comprehensive K–
11 12 coverage; requiring the Department of Education to
12 present recommended competencies for certain subject
13 area coverages to the state board by a specified date;
14 requiring the department to coordinate examinations
15 for such subject area coverages by a specified date;
16 providing an effective date.
