242 Section 1. Section 125.489, Florida Statutes, is created
243 to read:
244 125.489 Preemption of restrictions on gasoline-powered
245 farm equipment or gasoline-powered landscape equipment.—
246 (1) As used in this section, the term:
247 (a) "Gasoline-powered farm equipment" means any machine
248 powered by an internal combustion engine or a motor that uses
249 gasoline, diesel, or a blend of gasoline and oil which is used
250 on a farm or used to transport farm products.

251 (b) "Gasoline-powered landscape equipment" means any
252 machine powered by an internal combustion engine or a motor that
253 uses gasoline, diesel, or a blend of gasoline and oil which is
254 used to provide landscape management or maintenance or to move
255 leaves, dirt, grass, or other debris off of sidewalks,
256 driveways, lawns, or other surfaces.
257 (2) A county may not enact or enforce a resolution, an
258 ordinance, a rule, a code, or a policy or take any action that
259 restricts or prohibits the use of gasoline-powered farm
260 equipment or gasoline-powered landscape equipment and may not
261 create differing standards for such equipment or distinguish
262 such equipment from any electric or similar equipment in a
263 retail, manufacturer, or distributor setting.
264 (3) This section does not prohibit or limit a county from
265 encouraging the use of alternative farm or landscape equipment,
266 such as battery-powered farm or landscape equipment.
