1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to a Medicaid dental pilot program;
3 creating s. 409.9079, F.S.; defining the term "persons
4 with disabilities"; requiring the Agency for Health
5 Care Administration to establish and operate a 3-year
6 Medicaid dental pilot program in Region B of the
7 Statewide Medicaid Managed Care program for persons
8 with disabilities; providing eligibility criteria;
9 specifying covered benefits; providing reimbursement
10 requirements; requiring managed care participation;
11 directing the agency to seek federal approval;
12 requiring the agency to collect specified data and
13 submit a report to the Governor and Legislature by a
14 specified date; providing for expiration; providing an
15 effective date.
