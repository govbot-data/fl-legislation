2 A resolution designating August 10, 2026, as "Tay-

5 WHEREAS, Tay-Sachs disease is a genetic disorder caused by
6 the absence of beta-hexosaminidase A (Hex A), which causes cells
7 to become damaged, resulting in progressive neurological
8 disorders, and
9 WHEREAS, infants with Tay-Sachs disease often fail to
10 achieve milestones such as rolling over and sitting up, and
11 develop muscle weakness, which gradually leads to paralysis, and
12 WHEREAS, these infants also lose mental functions and
13 become increasingly unresponsive to their surroundings, in some
14 cases developing blindness, seizures, and difficulty swallowing
15 by 12 months of age, and
16 WHEREAS, children with Tay-Sachs disease often die by 4
17 years of age, and
18 WHEREAS, the carrier rate of Tay-Sachs disease for the
19 general population is 1 in 250 people, and a child has a 25
20 percent chance of having the disease when both parents are
21 carriers, and
22 WHEREAS, there is currently no treatment or cure for Tay-
23 Sachs disease, rather only ways to manage symptoms, and
24 WHEREAS, increasing awareness and education of Tay-Sachs
25 disease may lead to significant progress in the research to
