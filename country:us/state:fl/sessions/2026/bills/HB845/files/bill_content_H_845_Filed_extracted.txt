F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 845 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to employee child care assistance
3 programs study; creating s. 402.3216, F.S.; requiring
4 the Office of Program Policy Analysis and Government
5 Accountability, beginning on a specified date, to
6 perform a study of public and private employee child
7 care assistance programs; providing requirements for
8 the study; requiring the office to submit a certain
9 report to the Governor and Legislature by a specified
10 date; providing for future repeal of the study;
11 providing an effective date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. Section 402.3216, Florida Statutes, is created
16 to read:
17 402.3216 Child Care Cost and Affordability Study.—
18 (1) Beginning July 1, 2026, the Office of Program Policy
19 Analysis and Government Accountability (OPPAGA) shall perform a
20 study of public and private child care assistance programs for
21 employees to evaluate the costs and affordability of the
22 programs and effects on school readiness. The study's scope must
23 include, but need not be limited to, all of the following:
24 (a) The characteristics of other states' programs.
25 (b) The potential benefits of a Florida program.
