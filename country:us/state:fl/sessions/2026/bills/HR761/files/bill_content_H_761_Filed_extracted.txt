F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HR 761 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution condemning the persecution of Christians
3 in Nigeria and supporting Nigeria's redesignation as a
4 Country of Particular Concern.
5
6 WHEREAS, Nigeria is experiencing the highest levels of
7 violence against Christians in the world since Boko Haram's
8 insurgency began in 2009, and
9 WHEREAS, according to the International Society for Civil
10 Liberties and Rule of Law, since 2009 approximately 52,250
11 Christians have been brutally murdered in Nigeria, more than
12 anywhere else in the world, and more than 19,000 churches have
13 been attacked or destroyed, and
14 WHEREAS, estimates indicate that between 50,000 and 100,000
15 Christians have been martyred for their faith since 2009, with
16 more than 7,000 Christians killed in 2025, an average of 35 per
17 day, and
18 WHEREAS, Boko Haram, the Islamic State West Africa
19 Province, and Fulani militants have systematically targeted
20 Christian communities through massacres, church burnings,
21 kidnappings, and sexual violence, leaving villages destroyed and
22 millions displaced, and
23 WHEREAS, more than 5 million Christians have been displaced
24 and forced into Internally Displaced Person or refugee camps in
25 Nigeria, and
