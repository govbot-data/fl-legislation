26 Section 1. Section 683.221, Florida Statutes, is created
27 to read:
28 683.221 Florida Speech and Debate Week.—
29 (1) February 1 through February 7 shall be annually
30 designated as "Florida Speech and Debate Week."
31 (2) Florida Speech and Debate Week shall be observed for
32 all of the following purposes:
33 (a) To recognize the academic, civic, and leadership value
34 of competitive speech and debate.
35 (b) To honor educators, coaches, judges, and school
36 districts that support debate programming.
37 (c) To inspire students statewide to participate in debate
38 as a pathway to civic engagement.
39 (d) To promote Florida's role as the national leader in
40 scholastic speech and debate.
41 (3) Public and charter schools, state agencies, and other
42 entities may observe Florida Speech and Debate Week.
