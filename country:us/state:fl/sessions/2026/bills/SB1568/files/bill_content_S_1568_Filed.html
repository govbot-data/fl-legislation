    4         Florida Stablecoin Pilot Program within the Department

    6         defining terms; authorizing the department to accept

    9         eligible payment stablecoins for refunds,

   13         participation in the pilot program is voluntary;
   14         authorizing applicants, licensees, or other

   17         participants to elect to receive refunds,

   19         payment stablecoins; requiring the department to

   21         specified purpose; authorizing the department to
   22         convert eligible payment stablecoins into United
   23         States currency and to credit an applicable licensing
