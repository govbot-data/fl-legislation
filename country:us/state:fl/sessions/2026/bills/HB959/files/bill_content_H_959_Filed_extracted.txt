HB 959 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to electronic payment processing fees
3 for motor vehicle purchases; creating s. 520.083,
4 F.S.; defining the term "electronic payment";
5 authorizing holders of retail installment contracts
6 and their agents to collect fees for processing
7 electronic payments made by motor vehicle retail
8 buyers under certain circumstances; providing an
9 effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Section 520.083, Florida Statutes, is created
14 to read:
15 520.083 Convenience fee for processing buyers' electronic
16 payments.—
17 (1) As used in this section, the term "electronic payment"
18 means a payment made by credit card, debit card, electronic
19 funds transfer, electronic check, or other electronic method.
20 (2) A holder of a retail installment contract or the
21 holder's agent may collect a fee for processing a retail buyer's
22 electronic payment under the retail installment contract if:
23 (a) The fee is reasonably related to the expense incurred
24 by the holder or holder's agent in processing the electronic
25 payment; and
