eliminating 4 the disproportionate down payment requirements that 5 uniquely burden Florida homeowners. 6 7 WHEREAS, the Federal National Mortgage Association, in its 8 2025 Selling Guide, establishes that attached units in 9 established condominium projects located in this state which are 10 principal residences are subject to a maximum loan-to-value 11 (LTV) ratio of 75 percent under a lender-limited review, and 12 WHEREAS, that same guide allows attached units in 13 established condominium projects located outside of this state 14 to qualify for financing with a maximum LTV ratio WHEREAS, the United States recognizes that equal access to 34 credit is a cornerstone of fair housing, and the Civil Rights 35 Act of 1866 guarantees equal rights to purchase and sell real 36 property, and 37 WHEREAS, the Fair Housing Act, the Equal Credit Opportunity 38 Act, the Federal National Mortgage Association, and the Home 39 Mortgage Disclosure Act prohibit discrimination in
