HB 1313 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to surrendering to law enforcement;
3 creating s. 901.155, F.S.; providing definitions;
4 requiring a law enforcement agency to implement a
5 procedure for interviewing a person who attempts to be
6 taken into custody by such agency for a violation of
7 criminal law when no warrant for such person's arrest
8 has been issued; specifying that such procedure shall
9 include certain actions; authorizing a temporary
10 detention in certain circumstances; authorizing a
11 warrantless arrest in certain circumstances; requiring
12 a law enforcement officer to deliver such person or
13 have him or her delivered to the nearest receiving
14 facility for involuntary examination in certain
15 circumstances; providing an effective date.
16
