   11         WHEREAS, the organization undertakes the critical work of

   14  state and the nation, and
   15         WHEREAS, The Ripple Project tirelessly commits its

   22  a brighter, cancer-free future for all children, and
   23         WHEREAS, the Florida Senate wishes to commend and celebrate

   25  Project as it represents the very best of community action and
   26  philanthropic endeavor in the capital city, NOW, THEREFORE,
   27
