CS/HB 387 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to automatic dependent surveillance
3 broadcasts; creating s. 330.42, F.S.; providing
4 definitions; prohibiting airports from using
5 information broadcast or collected by automatic
6 dependent surveillance broadcast systems for specified
7 purposes under certain conditions; providing an
8 effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. Section 330.42, Florida Statutes, is created to
13 read:
14 330.42 Automatic dependent surveillance broadcasts.—
15 (1) For purposes of this section, the term:
16 (a) "Aircraft" has the same meaning as in s. 330.27,
17 except that the aircraft must have a gross weight of 12,499
18 pounds or less and operate under 14 C.F.R. part 91.
19 (b) "Automatic dependent surveillance broadcast" means an
20 advanced aviation surveillance technology that combines an
21 aircraft's positioning source, aircraft avionics, and a ground
22 infrastructure to create an accurate surveillance interface
23 between an aircraft and air traffic control. The term includes
24 two different services, ADS-B In and ADS-B Out, that can provide
25 information, such as an aircraft's global positioning system
