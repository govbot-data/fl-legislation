F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
CS/HB 839 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to unlicensed practice of engineering;
3 amending s. 471.033, F.S.; providing penalties for
4 persons found to have repeatedly engaged in the
5 unlicensed practice of engineering; providing an
6 effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. Present subsection (4) of section 471.033,
11 Florida Statutes, is redesignated as subsection (5), and a new
12 subsection (4) is added to that section, to read:
13 471.033 Disciplinary proceedings.—
14 (4) Notwithstanding subsection (3), a person who violates
15 this section by engaging in the unlicensed practice of
16 engineering, upon a second or subsequent violation, is subject
17 to a fine imposed by the department which increases as follows:
18 (a) For a second violation, a fine of $10,000.
19 (b) For a third violation, a fine of $15,000.
20 (c) For a fourth violation, a fine of $20,000.
21 (d) For a fifth and any subsequent violation, a fine of
22 $25,000.
23 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
