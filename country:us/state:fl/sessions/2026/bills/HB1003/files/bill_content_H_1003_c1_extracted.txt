1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to open cannabis containers in motor
3 vehicles; creating s. 316.19361, F.S.; providing
4 definitions; prohibiting a person from possessing an
5 open cannabis container while operating or a passenger
6 in or on a motor vehicle or while seated in or on a
7 motor vehicle that is parked or stopped within a road;
8 specifying circumstances in which an open cannabis
9 container is considered to be in a person's
10 possession; providing applicability; providing
11 penalties; requiring the Department of Highway Safety
12 and Motor Vehicles to report certain violations to the
13 Department of Health and requiring the Department of
14 Health to maintain records of such violations;
15 authorizing a local government to adopt certain
16 ordinances; providing construction; amending s.
17 381.986, F.S.; requiring the Department of Health to
18 suspend or revoke the registration of a qualified
19 patient or caregiver under specified circumstances;
20 providing an effective date.

26 316.19361 Possession of open cannabis containers in
27 vehicles prohibited; penalties.—
28 (1) As used in this section, the term:
29 (a) "Caregiver" has the same meaning as in s. 381.986(1).
30 (b) "Marijuana" means all parts of any plant of the genus
31 Cannabis, whether growing or not; the seeds thereof; the resin
32 extracted from any part of the plant; and every compound,
33 manufacture, salt, derivative, mixture, or preparation of the
34 plant or its seeds or resin, including edibles, which are
35 dispensed from a medical marijuana treatment center for medical
36 use by a qualified patient. The term does not include low-THC
37 cannabis. As used in this paragraph, the terms "edibles," "low-
38 THC cannabis," and "medical use" have the same meanings as in s.
39 381.986(1).
40 (c) "Open cannabis container" means a container that
41 contains marijuana and from which a person can immediately
42 consume, inhale, or smoke such marijuana.
43 (d) "Qualified patient" has the same meaning as in s.
44 381.986(1).
45 (e) "Road" has the same meaning as in s. 316.1936(1).
46 (2) It is unlawful for any person to possess an open
47 cannabis container while he or she is:
48 (a) Operating a motor vehicle in this state or while a
49 passenger in or on a motor vehicle being operated in this state.
50 (b) Seated in or on a motor vehicle that is parked or
