1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to commercial service airports;
3 amending s. 332.0075, F.S.; defining the term "major

5 major commercial service airports to the state;
6 providing names for major commercial service airports;
7 providing that such airport names continue to be valid
8 under certain circumstances; requiring the Department
9 of Transportation to annually review provisions naming
10 major commercial service airports for a certain

14 created on or after a certain date use such airport
15 names; providing an effective date.
