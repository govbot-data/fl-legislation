1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the one-cent piece; amending s.
3 212.12, F.S.; authorizing dealers to round to the
4 nearest nickel in certain circumstances; specifying
5 the methodology for such rounding; providing
6 applicability and construction; amending s. 501.212,
7 F.S.; providing that rounding to the nearest nickel is
8 not a deceptive and unfair trade practice in certain
9 circumstances; amending s. 538.235, F.S.; requiring
10 that certain cash payments be made in the full amount
11 due or rounded in a specified manner in certain
12 circumstances; providing an effective date.
