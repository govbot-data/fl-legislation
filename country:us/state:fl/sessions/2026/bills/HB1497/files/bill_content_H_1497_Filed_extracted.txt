1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the My Safe Florida Condominium
3 Pilot Program; amending s. 215.55871, F.S.; defining
4 the term "area median income"; removing the definition
5 of the term "service area"; revising eligibility
6 requirements for the My Safe Florida Condominium Pilot
7 Program; requiring the Department of Financial
8 Services to adopt rules to verify household income;
9 authorizing condominiums with mixed-income occupancies
10 to participate in the pilot program under certain
11 circumstances; requiring an application for a
12 mitigation grant to include documentation to verify
13 household income; limiting the award of grant funds;
14 requiring an association to complete a certain
15 percentage of opening protection improvements;
16 providing an effective date.
