1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to prohibited uses of public funds by

11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

13 Section 1. Section 112.3662, Florida Statutes, is created
14 to read:
15 112.3662 Prohibiting political subdivision from using
16 public funds to fund certain entities and organizations.—
17 (1) As used in this section, the term:
18 (a) "Not-for-profit entity or organization" means a
19 Florida corporation, not for profit, incorporated under chapter
20 617, and approved by the Secretary of State.
21 (b) "Political subdivision" has the same meaning as in s.
22 112.3121.
23 (2) A political subdivision may not use public funds to
24 fund any not-for-profit entity or organization in this state.
