27 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
28
29 Section 1. This act may be cited as the "Joe Casello Act."
30 Section 2. Section 376.911, Florida Statutes, is created
31 to read:
32 376.911 Aqueous film-forming foam.—
33 (1) As used in this section, the term:
34 (a) "Aqueous film-forming foam" means any firefighting
35 foam containing intentionally added perfluoroalkyl and
36 polyfluoroalkyl substances, including perfluorooctanoic acid and
37 perfluorooctane sulfonate as defined by the United States
38 Environmental Protection Agency.
39 (b) "Department" means the Department of Environmental
40 Protection.
41 (2) Effective July 1, 2026:
42 (a) Aqueous film-forming foam may not be used for any
43 nonemergency instruction, training, or testing.
44 (b) All entities in possession of aqueous film-forming
45 foam must report inventories to the department.
46 (3) Effective July 1, 2027:
47 (a) The sale, purchase, or distribution of aqueous film-
48 forming foam within this state is prohibited.
49 (b) All entities with remaining inventories of aqueous
50 film-forming foam must submit a disposal plan to the department.
