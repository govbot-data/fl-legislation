1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl
3 substances; providing a short title; creating s.
4 376.911, F.S.; defining the term "aqueous film-forming
5 foam"; prohibiting, beginning on a specified date,
6 certain use and the sale, purchase, or distribution of
7 aqueous film-forming foam; requiring, beginning on a
8 specified date, certain entities to submit aqueous
9 film-forming foam inventories and disposal plans to
10 the Department of Environmental Protection;
11 prohibiting, beginning on a specified date, the
12 possession and use of aqueous film-forming foam;
13 providing applicability; providing duties of the
14 department; authorizing the department to administer
15 certain grants or cost share programs; providing
16 penalties and injunctive relief; amending s. 403.086,
17 F.S.; requiring certain public entities disposing of
18 domestic wastewater biosolids and treated effluent to
19 quarterly conduct specified samplings and submit the
20 results to the Department of Environmental Protection;
21 providing an effective date.

26 Section 2. Section 376.911, Florida Statutes, is created
27 to read:
28 376.911 Aqueous film-forming foam.—
29 (1) As used in this section, the term "aqueous film-
30 forming foam" means any firefighting foam containing
31 intentionally added perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl
32 substances, including perfluorooctanoic acid and perfluorooctane
33 sulfonate as defined by the United States Environmental
34 Protection Agency.
35 (2) Effective July 1, 2026:
36 (a) Aqueous film-forming foam may not be used for any
37 nonemergency instruction, training, or testing.
38 (b) All entities in possession of aqueous film-forming
39 foam must report inventories to the department.
40 (3) Effective July 1, 2027:
41 (a) The sale, purchase, or distribution of aqueous film-
42 forming foam within this state is prohibited.
43 (b) All entities with remaining inventories of aqueous
44 film-forming foam must submit a disposal plan to the department.
45 (4) Effective July 1, 2028, possession and use of aqueous
46 film-forming foam is prohibited in this state.
47 (5) The prohibition under subsection (4) does not apply
48 to:
49 (a) Federal aviation facilities required to comply with 14
50 C.F.R. part 139.
