1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to trust funds; re-creating the
3 Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund within the
4 Executive Office of the Governor; amending s.
5 252.3711, F.S.; revising and providing uses of moneys
6 deposited in the fund; providing requirements for the
7 deposit of federal reimbursements of state emergency
8 expenditures; providing reporting requirements;
9 revising provisions relating to the termination of the
10 fund; providing for retroactive application; providing
11 an effective date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. The Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund
16 within the Executive Office of the Governor, which is to be
17 terminated pursuant to Section 19(f), Article III of the State
18 Constitution on February 17, 2026, is re-created.
19 Section 2. Section 252.3711, Florida Statutes, is amended
20 to read:

22 (1) The Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund is
23 created within the Executive Office of the Governor.
24 (2) The fund is established for use as a depository for
