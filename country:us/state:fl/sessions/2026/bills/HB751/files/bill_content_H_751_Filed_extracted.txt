HB 751 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to infrastructure and resiliency;
3 amending s. 255.065, F.S.; revising the definition of
4 the term "qualifying project"; creating s. 380.0934,
5 F.S.; providing definitions; providing that the
6 Department of Environmental Protection has the
7 exclusive authority to execute coastal resiliency
8 projects through public-private partnerships;
9 authorizing the department to take certain actions to
10 encourage investment from the private sector in
11 coastal resiliency projects; requiring the department
12 to publish certain information on its website;
13 providing an effective date.

51 (1) As used in this section, the term:
52 (a) "Coastal resiliency project" means:
53 1. Planning, contracting, and executing a project to
54 address flooding and sea level rise in a coastal or inland
55 community in this state under s. 380.093(5);
56 2. Public infrastructure repair and upgrades to seawalls
57 and stormwater drainage; and
58 3. Resiliency measures designed to withstand extreme
59 weather, mitigate flooding, and prevent coastal erosion,
60 including:
61 a. Acquisition of at-risk coastal and flood-prone
62 properties;
63 b. Acquisition of properties in areas at high risk of
64 flooding;
65 c. Infrastructure hardening and development of natural
66 barriers;
67 d. Construction of large-scale seawalls, levees, and
68 elevated flood barriers; or
69 e. Expansion and restoration of natural protective
70 systems.
71 (b) "Department" means the Department of Environmental
72 Protection.
73 (c) "Public-private partnership" means a coastal
74 resiliency project entered into by the department under s.
75 255.065.

76 (2) The department has the exclusive authority to execute
77 coastal resiliency projects through public-private partnerships
78 under s. 255.065.
79 (3) To encourage investment from the private sector in
80 coastal resiliency projects, the department may:
81 (a) Enter into long-term revenue-sharing agreements.
82 (b) Provide expedited permitting for construction.
83 (c) Seek comments from local governments and the public
84 during project planning and execution and incorporate actions
85 responsive to such comments into the project.
86 (d) Engage in-state vocational schools and apprenticeship
87 programs to train workers in specialized resiliency
88 construction.
89 (4) The department shall publish on its website biennial
90 progress reports for each coastal resiliency project funded
91 through a public-private partnership, including project
92 milestones, expenditures, and public benefits. The department
93 shall also create and maintain on its website an online
94 dashboard for real-time updates on project execution.
95 Section 3. This act shall take effect upon becoming a law.
