F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 573 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to discounted drug pricing study;
3 requiring the Agency for Health Care Administration to
4 conduct or contract for a specified study; providing
5 requirements for such study; requiring the agency to
6 provide the results to the Governor and the
7 Legislature by a specified date; providing an
8 effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. (1) The Agency for Health Care Administration
13 shall conduct or contract for a detailed fiscal impact study to
14 determine the feasibility of implementing the following
15 provisions relating to discounted drug prices under the 340B
16 Drug Pricing Program:
17 (a)1. Requiring drug manufacturers to sell a drug to
18 Medicaid pharmacies at not more than the 340B Drug Pricing
19 Program discounted price if the drug is:
20 a. On the Medicaid preferred drug list as described in s.
21 409.912(5), Florida Statutes.
22 b. A covered drug under the 340B Drug Pricing Program.
23 2. Authorizing drug manufacturers to deliver drugs sold in
24 accordance with subparagraph 1. to the purchasing Medicaid
25 pharmacy by any method used by the drug manufacturer.
