7 WHEREAS, Florida is home to nearly 1.2 million proud Puerto

18 the Jones-Shafroth Act in 1917, contributing to the historical
19 ties between Puerto Rico and the United States, and
20 WHEREAS, Puerto Rico, as a Commonwealth of the United
21 States since 1952, holds a unique status that contributes to its
22 distinctive national identity, and

24 Regiment, known as the "Borinqueneers," during various
