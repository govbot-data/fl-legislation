54 331.3052 Powers and duties of spaceport operators.—
55 (1) Notwithstanding any other provision of this part,
56 authority over the operational oversight, management, and day-
57 to-day administration of each spaceport shall be vested in the
58 spaceport director or commander for that facility.
59 (2) Space Florida shall be responsible for statewide
60 strategic planning, financing, economic development, and
61 intergovernmental coordination, but may not exercise direct
62 control over local operational decisions unless:
63 (a) Specifically requested in writing by the spaceport
64 director or commander; or
65 (b) Required to comply with applicable federal law or
66 regulation.
67 (3) Each spaceport shall develop and maintain an
68 operational governance plan consistent with Space Florida's
69 statewide strategic plan. Such plan must be submitted annually
70 to Space Florida, but is not subject to approval by Space
71 Florida.
72 (4) Each spaceport shall maintain an updated list of
73 spaceport site readiness development projects consistent with
74 its operational governance plan and with Space Florida's
75 spaceport master plan developed under s. 331.360. Such projects
