11 Section 1. Section 491.019, Florida Statutes, is created
12 to read:
13 491.019 Licensure by endorsement; marriage and family
14 therapists.—The board shall issue a license to practice marriage
15 and family therapy to an applicant who meets all of the
16 following criteria:
17 (1) Submits a complete application.
18 (2) Submits the required application fee.
19 (3) Holds an active, unencumbered marriage and family
20 therapy license in good standing issued by another state, the
21 District of Columbia, or a territory of the United States and
22 provides proof of all mental health licenses held in any other
23 jurisdiction.
24 (4) Attests that he or she is not, at the time of
25 submission of the application, the subject of a disciplinary
