187 (4) LIMITATIONS ON DAMAGES.—The Legislature is cognizant
188 of the increasing costs of goods and services each year and
189 recognizes that fixing a set amount of compensation has the
190 effect of a reduction in compensation each year. Accordingly,
191 the conditional limitations on damages in this section shall be
192 increased at the rate of 5 percent each year, prorated from July
193 1, 2014, to the date at which damages subject to such
194 limitations are awarded by final judgment or settlement.
195 Section 2. When determining liability limits for a claim
196 under this act, the limitations of liability in effect on the
197 date the claim accrues shall apply to the claim.
