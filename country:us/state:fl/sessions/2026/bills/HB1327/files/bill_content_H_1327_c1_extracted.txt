F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
CS/HB 1327 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to veteran benefit payments to minor
3 clients; amending s. 402.33, F.S.; requiring the
4 Department of Children and Families to receive,
5 deposit, and conserve certain benefit payments for
6 certain minor clients; providing requirements for such
7 funds; prohibiting the Department of Children and
8 Families from using such funds for certain purposes;
9 providing an effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Subsections (3) through (9) of section 402.33,
14 Florida Statutes, are renumbered as subsections (4) through
15 (10), respectively, and a new subsection (3) is added to that
16 section, to read:
17 402.33 Department authority to charge fees for services
18 provided.—
19 (3) The Department of Children and Families must receive,
20 deposit, and conserve all survivor benefit payments provided by
21 the United States Department of Veterans' Affairs on behalf of a
22 minor client in out-of-home care. The accumulated funds must be
23 disbursed in their entirety to the minor client upon attaining
24 18 years of age. The Department of Children and Families may not
25 use such funds to reimburse the state for the cost of care.
