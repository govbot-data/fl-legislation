51 560.103 Definitions.—As used in this chapter, the term:
52 (13) "Custodian of gold coin or silver coin," or
53 "custodian," as used for purposes of transactional gold or
54 silver, "Custodian of gold coin or silver coin" or "custodian"
55 means any person or entity providing secure vault facilities to
56 one or more persons for the safekeeping and storage of gold coin
57 or silver coin, the ownership of which is intended to be able to
58 or may be transferred electronically as defined in s. 215.986(1)
59 for legal tender. The term includes any person who holds gold
60 coin or silver coin for more than 10 days. The term does not
61 include a person who holds gold coin or silver coin for personal
62 use as legal tender, or a person or entity that holds gold or
63 silver that is not intended to be used as legal tender.
64 Section 4. This act shall take effect upon becoming a law.
