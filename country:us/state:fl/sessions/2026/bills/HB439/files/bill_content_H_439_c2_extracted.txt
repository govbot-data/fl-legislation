79 Section 2. Section 460.4086, Florida Statutes, is created
80 to read:
81 460.4086 Administration of sterile substances by
82 chiropractic physicians.—
83 (1) A chiropractic physician must be certified before
84 administering sterile substances by means of injection to a
85 patient.
86 (2) The board shall establish minimum standards of
87 practice for the administration of sterile substances by means
88 of injection by chiropractic physicians, including, at a
89 minimum, all of the following:
90 (a) Completion of 36 hours of continuing education
91 consisting of didactic lectures and laboratory training on the
92 safe and effective administration of vitamins, minerals, and
93 nutritional supplements, including, but not limited to,
94 protocols, indications, and contraindications.
95 (b) Passage of a written and practical examination.
96 (c) A requirement that the administration of sterile
97 substances by means of injection may not be performed without
98 patient consent and education on the risks and adverse events
99 that could occur. Such patient consent and education must be
100 included as part of the patient's documented plan of care.
