F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 713 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to locations of sheriffs' offices;
3 amending s. 30.10, F.S.; authorizing sheriffs' offices
4 to be located at any location in the counties, rather
5 than requiring the offices to be at county seats;
6 providing an effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. Section 30.10, Florida Statutes, is amended to
11 read:
12 30.10 Place of office.—The place of office of every
13 sheriff may shall be at any location within the county lines
14 seat of the county.
15 Section 2. This act shall take effect upon becoming a law.
