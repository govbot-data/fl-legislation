HR 8009 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution recognizing March 21, 2026, as "Persian
3 New Year Day" in Florida, celebrating the historical
4 and cultural significance of Nowruz, and expressing
5 appreciation for the contributions of Iranian
6 Americans.
7
8 WHEREAS, Nowruz, the traditional Persian New Year, has been
9 celebrated for more than 3,000 years, dating back to ancient
10 Persia, and
11 WHEREAS, Nowruz, or "New Day," which marks the arrival of
12 spring, is celebrated on the day of the vernal equinox, and
13 WHEREAS, the creation of the Haft-seen table, a symbolic
14 spread of seven elements representing beauty, health, patience,
15 spring, fertility, rebirth, and prosperity, is an important part
16 of the Nowruz tradition, and
17 WHEREAS, Nowruz is celebrated by hundreds of millions of
18 Iranians and other persons all over the world, including as
19 Novruz in Azerbaijan, Navruz in Uzbekistan, and Nowruz in
20 Afghanistan, as well as by Iranian Americans of all spiritual
21 belief in the United States, including those of Baha'i,
22 Christian, Jewish, Muslim, or Zoroastrian faiths and those with
23 nonreligious backgrounds, and
24 WHEREAS, members of the Iranian American community, many of
25 whom have made a lasting impact on the social and economic
