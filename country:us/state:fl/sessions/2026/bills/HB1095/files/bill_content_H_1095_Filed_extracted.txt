1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to salaries of the clerks of the

9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

11 Section 1. Subsection (1) of section 145.051, Florida
12 Statutes, is amended to read:
13 145.051 Clerk of circuit court; county comptroller.—
14 (1) Each clerk of the circuit court and each county
15 comptroller shall receive as salary the amount indicated, based
16 on the population of his or her county. In addition, a
17 compensation shall be made for population increments over the
18 minimum for each population group, which shall be determined by
19 multiplying the population in excess of the minimum for the
20 group times the group rate.
