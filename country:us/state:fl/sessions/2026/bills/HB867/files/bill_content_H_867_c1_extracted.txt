29 468.227 Occupational therapist; performance of dry
30 needling.—
31 (1) The board shall establish minimum standards of
32 practice for the performance of dry needling by occupational
33 therapists, including, but not limited to, all of the following:
34 (a) Completion of 2 years of licensed practice as an
35 occupational therapist.
36 (b) Completion of 50 hours of face-to-face continuing
37 education from an entity approved by the board on the topic of
38 dry needling, which must include a determination by the
39 instructor that the occupational therapist demonstrates the
40 requisite psychomotor skills needed to safely perform dry
41 needling. The continuing education must include instruction in
42 all of the following areas:
43 1. Theory of dry needling.
44 2. Selection and safe handling of needles and other
45 apparatus or equipment used in dry needling, including
46 instruction on the proper handling of biohazardous waste.
47 3. Indications and contraindications for dry needling.
48 4. Psychomotor skills needed to safely perform dry
49 needling.
50 5. Postintervention care, including adverse responses,
