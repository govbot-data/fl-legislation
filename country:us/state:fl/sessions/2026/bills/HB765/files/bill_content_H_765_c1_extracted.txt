427 (1) PURPOSE AND INTENT.—The Legislature recognizes that

429 a vital role in providing child care in Florida. It is the
430 intent of the Legislature that residential property insurance

434 liability of residential property insurers is substantially

437 need to specify that contractual liabilities that arise in
