32 Section 1. Subsection (7) is added to section 320.06,
33 Florida Statutes, to read:
34 320.06 Registration certificates, license plates, and
35 validation stickers generally.—
36 (7) The department shall establish and maintain a secure
37 online license and registration portal accessible to the
38 department, tax collectors, and authorized law enforcement
39 agencies for the purpose of verifying, recording, and processing
40 the seizure or confiscation of license plates under this
41 chapter. The portal must provide immediate status updates when a
42 plate has been seized, destroyed, or returned and must be
43 checked before reissuance of a registration or plate. The
44 department may adopt rules to implement this subsection.
