HR 8127 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating March 2026 as "Autoimmune
3 Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, researchers have identified more than 140
6 autoimmune diseases, many of which are chronic, debilitating,
7 and currently have no known cure, and
8 WHEREAS, autoimmune diseases can affect nearly every part
9 of the human body, including the blood, blood vessels, muscles,
10 nervous system, gastrointestinal tract, endocrine glands, and
11 multiple organ systems, and can be life-threatening, and
12 WHEREAS, autoimmune diseases display a high degree of
13 comorbidity within individuals and families, suggesting shared
14 risk factors, and
15 WHEREAS, autoimmune diseases have a major impact on the
16 individuals and families they affect, as well as our society and
17 health care costs, and current projections suggest that
18 autoimmune diseases may soon take their place among the
19 predominant medical disorders, and
20 WHEREAS, approximately 8 percent of the United States
21 population is living with an autoimmune disease, and nearly 80
22 percent of those with an autoimmune disease are women, and
23 WHEREAS, autoimmune diseases in children are rare and they
24 can be challenging to diagnose and difficult to treat, and

25 WHEREAS, diagnosing autoimmune conditions remains
26 difficult, with studies indicating that up to 76 percent of
27 patients receive at least one incorrect diagnosis before the
28 underlying cause is identified, and
29 WHEREAS, delays in diagnosis can be frustrating and take a
30 toll on both physical and mental health and can also result in
31 significant harm, and
32 WHEREAS, the Autoimmune Association has reported that it
33 takes on average 4.5 years for a patient to receive an
34 autoimmune disease diagnosis, and
35 WHEREAS, "Autoimmune Awareness Month" serves as a valuable
36 opportunity to help educate the public about autoimmune
37 diseases, support individuals and families, and promote greater
38 understanding of these diseases, NOW, THEREFORE,
39
40 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of
41 Florida:
42
43 That March 2026 is designated as "Autoimmune Awareness
44 Month" in Florida.
