1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to school districts; creating s.
3 1001.366, F.S.; providing members of a district school
4 board with specified rights relating to school
5 district documents and budget matters; providing
6 requirements for the provision of such school district
7 documents to district school board members; amending
8 s. 1001.42, F.S.; requiring that certain documents
9 from district school board meetings be kept as public
10 records; amending s. 1011.035, F.S.; requiring that
11 full line-item budget items be posted on a school
12 district's website; amending s. 1012.22, F.S.;
13 requiring district school boards to adopt a policy
14 prohibiting a requirement that district employees be
15 required or incentivized to sign a nondisclosure
16 agreement or confidentiality agreement; providing an
17 effective date.
