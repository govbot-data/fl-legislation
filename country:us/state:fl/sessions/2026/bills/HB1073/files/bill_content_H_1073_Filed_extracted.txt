1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to school districts; amending s.
3 112.3135, F.S.; revising the definition of the term
4 "agency" to include district school boards for
5 purposes of provisions restricting the employment of
6 relatives of public officials; creating s. 1001.366,
7 F.S.; providing members of a district school board
8 with specified rights relating to school district
9 documents and budget matters; amending s. 1001.42,
10 F.S.; requiring that certain documents from district
11 school board meetings be kept as public records;
12 amending s. 1011.035, F.S.; requiring that full line-
13 item budget items be posted on a school district's
14 website; amending s. 1012.22, F.S.; defining the term
15 "good cause"; amending s. 1015.03, F.S.; providing
16 that a school district employee may not be required or
17 incentivized to sign a nondisclosure agreement or
18 confidentiality agreement; providing an effective
19 date.
