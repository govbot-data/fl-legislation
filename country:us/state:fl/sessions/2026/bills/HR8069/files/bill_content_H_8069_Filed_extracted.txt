HR 8069 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating September 2026 as "Childhood
3 Cancer Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, almost 15,000 children are diagnosed with cancer
6 in the United States each year, and
7 WHEREAS, approximately 50 children are diagnosed with
8 cancer each day and as of 2023 there are roughly 500,000
9 survivors of childhood and adolescent cancer in the United
10 States, and
11 WHEREAS, each year, many children in Florida face a cancer
12 diagnosis which impacts parents, grandparents, loved ones,
13 friends, neighbors, and communities, and
14 WHEREAS, many infants, children, and teens suffer from the
15 long-term effects of comprehensive treatment, including
16 secondary cancers, and as many as two-thirds of childhood cancer
17 patients will experience late effects of chemotherapy or
18 radiation, which may persist or develop five years after
19 diagnosis, and
20 WHEREAS, although the incidence of childhood cancer has
21 been rising slightly in recent decades, survival rates continue
22 to improve as a result of improved treatment options, and
23 WHEREAS, the 5-year relative survival rate for childhood
24 cancer is higher than 80 percent; however, survival rates may
25 vary based on the type of cancer and the age of the patient, and
