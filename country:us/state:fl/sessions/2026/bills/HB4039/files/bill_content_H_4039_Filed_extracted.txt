HB 4039 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the solid waste disposal facility
3 in Broward County; defining the term "Monarch Hill
4 Landfill"; requiring a feasibility study and public
5 hearing before any expansion of the Monarch Hill
6 Landfill; providing requirements for the study and
7 public hearing; providing an effective date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11 Section 1. Monarch Hill Landfill; Expansion Requirements
12 in Broward County.
13 (1) Definition.—As used in this section, the term "Monarch
14 Hill Landfill" refers to the solid waste disposal facility
15 located in Broward County, currently operated under permits
16 issued by the Department of Environmental Protection.
17 (2) Feasibility Study Requirement.—Before issuing or
18 approving any application, zoning change, permit, or development
19 order that would allow for the physical expansion of the Monarch
20 Hill Landfill, Broward County must commission a feasibility
21 study, to be conducted by an independent third-party entity with
22 expertise in environmental engineering, solid waste management,
23 and public health impacts. The feasibility study must include,
24 but need not be limited to:
25 (a) Assessment of the environmental and public health
