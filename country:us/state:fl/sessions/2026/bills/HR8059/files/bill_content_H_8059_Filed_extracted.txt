HR 8059 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution recognizing the 175th anniversary of the
3 Young Men's Christian Association.
4
5 WHEREAS, for 175 years, the Young Men's Christian
6 Association or the "YMCA" has been the heartbeat of community,
7 dedicated to giving people of all ages, backgrounds, and walks
8 of life the opportunity to reach their full potential with
9 dignity, where strangers become friends and every generation
10 finds a place to belong, and
11 WHEREAS, in 1851, the YMCA was founded in the United States
12 by Captain Thomas Valentine Sullivan, an American seaman and
13 missionary, who was inspired by the YMCA movement in England,
14 with a mission to put Christian principles into practice through
15 programs that build healthy spirit, mind, and body for all,
16 guided by core values of caring, honesty, respect, and
17 responsibility, and
18 WHEREAS, from 1851 to today, the YMCA has continually
19 adapted to meet the evolving needs of American communities,
20 strengthening society by nurturing the potential of every child
21 and teen; improving the nation's health and well-being; bringing
22 people together across differences in age, income, and
23 background; fostering relationships; and creating a sense of
24 belonging, and

25 WHEREAS, today, the YMCA has nearly 2,600 locations
26 nationwide, serving more than 10,000 communities, with more than
27 350,000 volunteers, delivering various programs to over 17
28 million people, including almost 11 million adults and 6.5
29 million children and youth, and
30 WHEREAS, in Florida, the YMCA operates through multiple
31 associations serving communities across the state, where 1 out
32 of every 18 Florida residents is involved in the YMCA, providing
33 youth enrichment opportunities, promoting health and wellness,
34 building partnerships, and encouraging volunteerism, and
35 WHEREAS, the YMCA is a leader in providing childcare and
36 afterschool programs; swim lessons; camping; youth sports; meal
37 programs; and lifestyle health, fitness, and well-being
38 programs, NOW, THEREFORE,
39
40 Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of
41 Florida:
42
43 That the Young Men's Christian Association is recognized
44 for its 175 years of service to the nation and its continued
45 efforts to address social isolation and loneliness by creating
46 places and spaces that promote achievement, well-being, and
47 connection, helping people build relationships and thrive in
48 their communities.
