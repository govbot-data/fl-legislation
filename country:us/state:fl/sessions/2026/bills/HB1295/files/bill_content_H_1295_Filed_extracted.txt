101 Section 3. Section 430.71, Florida Statutes, is created to
102 read:
103 430.71 Florida Alzheimer's Center of Excellence.—
104 (1) PURPOSE AND INTENT.—The purpose of this section is to
105 assist and support persons with Alzheimer's disease or related
106 forms of dementia and their caregivers by connecting them with
107 resources in their communities to address two primary goals:
108 (a) To allow residents of this state living with
109 Alzheimer's disease or related forms of dementia to age in
110 place.
111 (b) To empower family caregivers to improve their own
112 well-being.
113 (2) DEFINITIONS.-As used in this section, the term:
114 (a) "Center" means the Florida Alzheimer's Center for
115 Excellence.
116 (b) "Department" means the Department of Elderly Affairs.
117 (3) POWERS AND DUTIES.-
118 (a) There is created within the department the Florida
119 Alzheimer's Center of Excellence, which shall assist in
120 improving the quality of care for persons living with
121 Alzheimer's disease or related forms of dementia and improved
122 quality of life for family caregivers. The center may contract
123 for services necessary to implement this section. The center
124 shall:
