1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to a review under the Open Government
3 Sunset Review Act; amending s. 119.0715, F.S., which
4 provides an exemption from public record requirements
5 for a trade secret held by an agency; removing the
6 scheduled repeal of the exemption; amending ss.
7 287.137, 288.075, 334.049, 408.185, 409.91196,
8 440.108, 497.172, 501.171, 501.1735, 501.2041,
9 501.722, 520.9965, 548.062, 559.5558, 569.215,
10 627.0628, and 1004.4472; removing references to trade
11 secrets from public records exemptions; providing an
12 effective date.
