HB 955 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to principal offices of limited
3 liability companies and corporations; amending ss.
4 605.0113 and 607.0501, F.S.; providing that a limited
5 liability company or corporation, foreign or domestic,
6 may designate its registered agent's address as the
7 principal office of such company or corporation;
8 requiring that certain limited liability companies or
9 corporations, foreign or domestic, provide the
10 Department of State with the physical address of a
11 named member or manager of such company or
12 corporation; providing an effective date.

20 (6) Each limited liability company and each foreign
21 limited liability company that does not have a principal office
22 as defined in s. 605.0102 may designate the address of the
23 limited liability company's registered agent as its principal
24 office.
