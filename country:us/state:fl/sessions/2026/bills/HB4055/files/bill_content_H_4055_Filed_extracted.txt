HB 4055 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the City of Lake Wales, Polk
3 County; providing an exception to general law for
4 licensed alcoholic beverage vendors located within a
5 specified area of the City of Lake Wales; providing
6 that licensed alcoholic beverage vendors within the
7 specified area must comply with all ordinances,
8 statutes, and rules not inconsistent with specified
9 provisions; defining the specified area of the city to
10 which such exception applies; providing an effective
11 date.
12
13 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
14
15 Section 1. Notwithstanding any other provision of the
16 Beverage Law as defined in s. 561.01(6), Florida Statutes, the
17 holder of any alcoholic beverage vendor license that permits
18 sales of alcoholic beverages for consumption on the premises,
19 and for which the designated premises of such license are
20 located on any property within the Lake Wales Arts, Culture and
21 Entertainment District of the City of Lake Wales, shall be
22 authorized to allow patrons to remove any alcoholic beverage,
23 whether sealed or in an open container, from the licensed vendor
24 premises for purposes of consumption away from the licensed
25 vendor premises within the district. Such licensed alcoholic
