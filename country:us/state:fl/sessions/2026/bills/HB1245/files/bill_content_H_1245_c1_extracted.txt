29 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

31 Section 1. Subsections (7) and (8) are added to section
32 403.0855, Florida Statutes, to read:
33 403.0855 Biosolids management.—
34 (7) The land application of bulk Class AA biosolids
35 fertilizer and compost products may not exceed the appropriate
36 agronomic rate. Application records must be maintained by the
37 land application site operator.
38 (8) The University of Florida's Institute of Food and
39 Agricultural Sciences shall, on a biennial basis, publish and
40 make publicly available the recommended agronomic rates for the
41 beneficial reuse of bulk Class AA biosolids fertilizer and
42 compost products based on predominant application practices.
