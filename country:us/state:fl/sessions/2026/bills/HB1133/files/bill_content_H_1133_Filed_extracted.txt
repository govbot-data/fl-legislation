HB 1133 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to pediatric behavioral and mental
3 health screenings; creating s. 409.9746, F.S.;
4 requiring the agency to adopt rules establishing
5 certain practice parameters and protocols for the
6 provision of early periodic screening, diagnostic, and
7 treatment services for certain persons; requiring the
8 Department of Health to provide a parent or legal
9 guardian information on sleep, screen time, and
10 nutrition and make such information available for
11 download; requiring the agency, by a specified date,
12 to adopt measures relating to early periodic
13 screening, diagnostic, and treatment services and
14 collect and report performance measures and outcome
15 data to the Governor and the Legislature; providing an
16 effective date.
