1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the South Indian River Water
3 Control District, Palm Beach County; amending chapter
4 2001-313, Laws of Florida, as amended; increasing the
5 number of members of the governing board; including
6 fractions of acres in all gross acreage owned by a
7 landowner; providing for compensation for supervisors
8 attending board meetings; requiring competitive
9 bidding for certain positions; revising residence
10 requirements for members of the board; providing for
11 staggering of terms, annual elections, and election of
12 board officers; requiring an election; removing
13 outdated language; requiring the board to authorize
14 the formation of an engineering plan under certain
15 circumstances; providing a limitation of a one-time
16 special assessment; authorizing district money to be
17 expended before the levy of such special assessment;
18 providing an effective date.

51 Section 3. Board of Supervisors; elections.—
52 (1) The Board of Supervisors of the South Indian River
53 Water Control District shall consist of six five members, at
54 least three of whom but not more than four of whom shall reside
55 in the District, at least one of whom shall reside to the East
56 of Canal 18, and three at least one of whom shall reside to the
57 West of Canal 18, and shall each be elected by the landowners of
58 each respective side of Canal 18. Immediately following the
59 enactment of this act, an election shall be held for the six
60 positions. The two candidates receiving the highest number of
61 votes from each area shall serve 3-year terms, the two
62 candidates receiving the second highest number of votes shall
63 serve 2-year terms, and the two candidates with the third
64 highest number of votes shall serve 1-year terms. Elections
65 shall occur annually thereafter as the initial terms expire. The
66 candidates with the highest number of votes in the subsequent
67 elections shall be elected for 3-year terms. In even-numbered
68 years, the members of the board elected from the area East of
69 Canal 18 shall elect the President, the Vice President, and the
70 Secretary for the Board that year.
