43 Section 2. The Legislature finds that a proper and
44 legitimate state purpose is served when employees, officers, and
45 retirees of the state and its political subdivisions, and the
46 dependents, survivors, and beneficiaries of such employees,
47 officers, and retirees, are extended the basic protections
48 afforded by governmental retirement systems. These persons must
49 be provided benefits that are fair and adequate and that are
50 managed, administered, and funded in an actuarially sound manner
51 as required by s. 14, Article X of the State Constitution and
52 part VII of chapter 112, Florida Statutes. Therefore, the
53 Legislature determines and declares that this act fulfills an
54 important state interest.
