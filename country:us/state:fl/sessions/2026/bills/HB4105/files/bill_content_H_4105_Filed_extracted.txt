HB 4105 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Port St. Joe Port Authority,
3 Franklin, Gadsden, Gulf, and Liberty Counties;
4 amending chapter 2000-488, Laws of Florida; expanding
5 the Port St. Joe Port Authority to include Franklin,
6 Gadsden, and Liberty Counties; revising membership of
7 the governing body of the authority; providing for
8 transition; providing for staggered terms; providing
9 an effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7 and subsection
14 (12) of 8 of section 3 of chapter 2000-488, Laws of Florida, are
15 amended to read:
16 Section 1. Creation; boundaries.—There is hereby created a
17 body corporate to be known as the Port St. Joe Port Authority
18 with the corporate powers to sue and be sued and with the
19 additional powers specified herein. Said authority shall be a
20 public agency for the development of commerce and the port of
21 Port St. Joe. The boundaries of the Port St. Joe Port Authority
22 shall embrace and include all of Franklin, Gadsden, Gulf, and
23 Liberty Counties.

74 Section 4. Powers.—The Port St. Joe Port Authority, as
75 hereby created, is hereby authorized and empowered:
76 (1) To own and acquire property or facilities by purchase,
77 lease, easement, eminent domain, gift, or transfer from the City
78 of Port St. Joe or from Franklin County, Gadsden County, Gulf
79 County, or Liberty County, the United States of America, the
80 State of Florida, any agencies thereof, corporations, or
81 individuals,
82 (2) To acquire, construct, maintain, and operate: port
83 terminal facilities; warehouses; wharves; docks; drydocks;
84 quays; yacht basins; bridges; ship basins; breakwaters;
85 foundation for shipways; fitting out docks; shipyards; marine
86 railways; railroads; repair shops; loading and unloading,
87 packaging and refrigeration facilities; and all other harbor,
88 port, commercial, and industrial improvements and facilities,
89 (3) To enter into contracts or leases with individuals,
90 corporations, any municipality, the State of Florida, and the
91 United States of America, or any other public body to operate
92 facilities owned or leased by said port authority,
93 (4) To enter into contracts for furthering the business,
94 operation, and maintenance of shipping facilities and harbor and
95 port improvements within the boundaries of the authority Gulf
96 County.
