2 An act relating to the Port St. Joe Port Authority,
3 Franklin, Gadsden, Gulf, and Liberty Counties;
4 amending chapter 2000-488, Laws of Florida; expanding

7 the governing body of the authority; providing for the
8 selection of a chair of the governing body; providing
9 for transition; providing for staggered terms;
10 providing an effective date.

14 Section 1. Sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7 and subsection

16 Florida, are amended to read:
17 Section 1. Creation; boundaries.—There is hereby created a
18 body corporate to be known as the Port St. Joe Port Authority
19 with the corporate powers to sue and be sued and with the
20 additional powers specified herein. Said authority shall be a
21 public agency for the development of commerce and the port of
22 Port St. Joe. The boundaries of the Port St. Joe Port Authority
23 shall embrace and include all of Franklin, Gadsden, Gulf, and
24 Liberty Counties.

77 Section 4. Powers.—The Port St. Joe Port Authority, as
78 hereby created, is hereby authorized and empowered:
79 (1) To own and acquire property or facilities by purchase,
80 lease, easement, eminent domain, gift, or transfer from the City
81 of Port St. Joe or from Franklin County, Gadsden County, Gulf
82 County, or Liberty County, the United States of America, the
83 State of Florida, any agencies thereof, corporations, or
84 individuals,
85 (2) To acquire, construct, maintain, and operate: port
86 terminal facilities; warehouses; wharves; docks; drydocks;
87 quays; yacht basins; bridges; ship basins; breakwaters;
88 foundation for shipways; fitting out docks; shipyards; marine

96 (4) To enter into contracts for furthering the business,
