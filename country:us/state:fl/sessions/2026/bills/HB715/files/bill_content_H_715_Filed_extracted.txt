1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to school and child care facility
3 emergency alerts; creating s. 784.072, F.S.; defining
4 terms; requiring a local law enforcement agency to
5 activate the emergency alert system and issue a Ya Ya
6 Alert to public and private schools and child care
7 facilities under certain circumstances; requiring
8 local law enforcement agencies to create and maintain
9 a list of such schools and facilities within their
10 jurisdictions; providing requirements for the
11 maintenance of such list; providing immunity from
12 civil liability to certain persons for damages for
13 complying in good faith with specified provisions;
14 providing a presumption of good faith; providing
15 construction; requiring the Department of Law
16 Enforcement, in cooperation with specified
17 departments, to activate the emergency alert system

22 civil liability to certain persons for damages for
23 complying in good faith with specified provisions;

29 Section 1. Section 784.072, Florida Statutes, is created

31 784.072 Notification of imminent threat to schools and
32 child care facilities or the public; Ya Ya Alert; Imminent
33 Threat Alert.—
34 (1) As used in this section, the term:
35 (a) "Child care facility" has the same meaning as in s.
36 402.302.
37 (b) "Imminent Threat Alert" means a notification issued
38 pursuant to subsection (3) which informs the public that an
39 imminent threat exists such that the lives and safety of people
40 are endangered, including, but not limited to, instances in
41 which a person suspected of killing or causing serious bodily
42 injury to another person or assaulting another person with a
43 deadly weapon has fled the scene of the offense.
44 (c) "Private school" has the same meaning as in s.
45 1002.01.
46 (d) "Ya Ya Alert" means a notification issued pursuant to
47 subsection (2) which informs public schools, private schools,
48 and child care facilities that an imminent threat exists,
49 including, but not limited to, instances in which a person
