1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating March 2026 as "Colorectal
3 Cancer Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, colorectal cancer is currently the second most
6 common cause of cancer deaths among men and women in the United
7 States when the numbers are combined, and
8 WHEREAS, one in 24 people will be diagnosed with colorectal
9 cancer in their lifetime, and
10 WHEREAS, each year, approximately 150,000 people are
11 diagnosed with, and an estimated 50,000 people will die from,
12 colorectal cancer in the United States, and
13 WHEREAS, according to the Department of Health, there were
14 37.8 colorectal cancer incidences per 100,000 Floridians, when
15 adjusted for age, in 2022, and
16 WHEREAS, regular screening, beginning at the age of 45, can
17 discover colorectal cancer in its earlier stages and may
18 increase colorectal cancer survival rates, and
19 WHEREAS, increasing awareness and education concerning
20 colorectal cancer can lead to significant progress in both
21 preventing and overcoming the disease as the majority of cases
22 have proven to be both treatable and survivable, and
23 WHEREAS, educational campaigns and efforts can provide
24 information to the public on methods of prevention and screening
25 and can help with early detection, and
