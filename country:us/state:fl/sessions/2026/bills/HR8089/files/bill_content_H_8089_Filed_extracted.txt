HR 8089 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution recognizing the second Monday in October
3 2026 as "Indigenous Peoples' Day" in the State of
4 Florida.
5
6 WHEREAS, Indigenous Peoples' Day is a holiday that
7 celebrates and honors Native American peoples and commemorates
8 their histories and cultures, and
9 WHEREAS, Indigenous Peoples' Day was first proposed in 1977
10 by a delegation of Native Nations to the United Nations
11 International Conference on Discrimination against Indigenous
12 Populations in the Americas, and
13 WHEREAS, Indigenous Peoples' Day is celebrated across the
14 United States on the second Monday of October and is an official
15 city and state holiday in various localities, and
16 WHEREAS, before colonization, indigenous peoples lived and
17 flourished in the Americas for thousands of years, and
18 WHEREAS, many native cultures were lost due to European
19 colonization of North America, which led to the deaths of
20 millions of native people and the forced assimilation of
21 survivors, and
22 WHEREAS, there are approximately 94,795 people identified
23 as Native Americans or as descendants of Native Americans living
24 in Florida based on 2020 United States Census Bureau data, and
