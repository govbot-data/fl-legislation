1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to boating-restricted areas; amending
3 s. 327.46, F.S.; authorizing municipalities and
4 counties to adopt ordinances to extend boating-
5 restricted areas under certain conditions; requiring
6 the boundaries of such areas to be clearly marked with
7 specified markers; providing an effective date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11 Section 1. Paragraph (c) of subsection (1) of section
12 327.46, Florida Statutes, is amended to read:
13 327.46 Boating-restricted areas.—
14 (1) Boating-restricted areas, including, but not limited
15 to, restrictions of vessel speeds and vessel traffic, may be
16 established on the waters of this state for any purpose
17 necessary to protect the safety of the public if such
18 restrictions are necessary based on boating accidents,
19 visibility, hazardous currents or water levels, vessel traffic
20 congestion, or other navigational hazards or to protect
21 seagrasses on privately owned submerged lands.
22 (c) Municipalities and counties may adopt have the
23 authority to establish by ordinance the following ordinances for
24 other boating-restricted areas:
