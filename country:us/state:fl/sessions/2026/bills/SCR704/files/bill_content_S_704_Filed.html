    1                    Senate Concurrent Resolution                   
    2         A concurrent resolution terminating the state of
    3         emergency and any order, proclamation, or rule issued
    4         or adopted thereunder declared by Executive Order 23
    5         03.
    6  
    7         WHEREAS, in response to the perceived threat of illegal
    8  immigration, on January 6, 2023, Governor Ron DeSantis issued
    9  Executive Order 23-03, and
   10         WHEREAS, Executive Order 23-03 declared a state of
   11  emergency to enable the state to address the major disaster of
   12  the migration of unauthorized aliens into this state, and
   13         WHEREAS, Governor Ron DeSantis has extended the declaration
   14  of a state of emergency by issuing subsequent executive orders
   15  as required, and
   16         WHEREAS, s. 252.36(3)(a), Florida Statutes, authorizes the
   17  Legislature to terminate, by concurrent resolution, a state of
   18  emergency declared by the Governor, NOW, THEREFORE,
   19  
   20  Be It Resolved by the Senate of the State of Florida, the House
   21  of Representatives Concurring:
   22  
   23         That, pursuant to the authority granted to the Legislature
   24  by s. 252.36(3)(a), Florida Statutes, the state of emergency
   25  declared by Executive Order 23-03, as extended, is terminated.
   26         BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that any order, proclamation, or
   27  rule issued or adopted pursuant to or in furtherance of
   28  Executive Order 23-03, as extended, is terminated.
   29         BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that, as required by s.
   30  252.36(3)(a), Florida Statutes, the Governor shall issue an
   31  executive order terminating the state of emergency declared by
   32  Executive Order 23-03, as extended.
