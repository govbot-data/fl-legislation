1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to services during a state of
3 emergency; amending s. 287.056, F.S.; authorizing the
4 Department of Management Services to enter into state
5 term contracts for the provision of building code
6 inspection services and debris removal services;
7 creating s. 468.634, F.S.; authorizing individuals to
8 work in specified positions if they meet certain
9 requirements; providing an effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Subsection (4) of section 287.056, Florida
14 Statutes, is renumbered as subsection (5), respectively, and a
15 new subsection (4) is added to that section, to read:
16 287.056 Purchases from purchasing agreements and state
17 term contracts; vendor disqualification.—
18 (4) For the purposes of recovery following a natural
19 disaster that is subject to a state of emergency declared by the
20 Governor, the department may enter into and maintain state term
21 contracts with multiple vendors for the purpose of performing
22 building code inspection services, as defined in s. 553.791, and
23 debris removal services, and make such services available to
24 local governments during a state of emergency.
