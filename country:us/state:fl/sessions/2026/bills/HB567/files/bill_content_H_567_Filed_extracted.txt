30 Section 2. Section 461.011, Florida Statutes, is created
31 to read:
32 461.011 Stem cell therapy.—
33 (1) The Legislature recognizes the significant potential
34 of stem cell therapies in advancing medical treatments and
35 improving patient outcomes and further recognizes the need to
36 ensure that such therapies are provided using stem cells
37 obtained in an ethical manner that does not involve stem cells
38 derived from aborted fetuses. It is the intent of the
39 Legislature to foster medical innovation while upholding ethical
40 standards that respect the sanctity of life. By encouraging the
41 use of stem cell sources such as adult stem cells, umbilical
42 cord blood, and other ethically obtained human cells, tissues,
43 or cellular or tissue-based products, the state will advance
44 regenerative medicine in a manner consistent with the values of
45 this state.
46 (2) As used in this section, the term:
47 (a) "Human cells, tissues, or cellular or tissue-based
48 products" means articles containing or consisting of human cells
49 or tissues that are intended for implantation, transplantation,
50 infusion, or transfer into a human recipient. The term does not
