333 Section 4. The Legislature finds that a proper and
334 legitimate state purpose is served when employees, officers, and
335 retirees of the state and its political subdivisions, and the
336 dependents, survivors, and beneficiaries of such employees,
337 officers, and retirees, are extended the basic protections
338 afforded by governmental retirement systems. These persons must

341 as required by s. 14, Article X of the State Constitution and
342 part VII of chapter 112, Florida Statutes. Therefore, the
343 Legislature determines and declares that this act fulfills an
344 important state interest.
