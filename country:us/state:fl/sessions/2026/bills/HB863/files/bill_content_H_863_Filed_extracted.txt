1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to arbitration for disputes with
3 Citizens Property Insurance Corporation; amending s.
4 627.351, F.S.; requiring notification of arbitration
5 before the Division of Administrative Hearings as an
6 option for dispute resolution procedures under
7 Citizens Property Insurance Corporation; removing
8 obsolete language; providing an effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. Paragraph (ll) of subsection (6) of section
13 627.351, Florida Statutes, is amended to read:
14 627.351 Insurance risk apportionment plans.—
15 (6) CITIZENS PROPERTY INSURANCE CORPORATION.—
16 (ll)1. In addition to any other method of alternative
17 dispute resolution authorized by state law, the corporation may
18 adopt policy forms that provide an option for the insured to
19 select, at the time of entering into the policy or upon renewal,
20 to have disputes regarding the corporation's claim
21 determinations for the resolution of disputes regarding its
22 claim determinations, including disputes regarding coverage for,
23 or the scope and value of, a claim, resolved through arbitration
24 in a proceeding before the Division of Administrative Hearings.
