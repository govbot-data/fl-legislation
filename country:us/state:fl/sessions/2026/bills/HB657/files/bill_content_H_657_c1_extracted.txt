87 Section 1. Section 712.13, Florida Statutes, is created to
88 read:
89 712.13 Governing documents associated with dissolved
90 homeowners' associations.—
91 (1) The Legislature finds that when a homeowners'
92 association has been dissolved, voluntarily or involuntarily,
93 the recorded governing documents created solely for the
94 association's benefit may remain in the county records
95 indefinitely, imposing an undue burden on private property
96 owners. It is the intent of the Legislature to restore and
97 protect private property rights by ensuring that such
98 encumbrances are properly extinguished or removed from the
99 official records of the county when the association ceases to
100 exist.
