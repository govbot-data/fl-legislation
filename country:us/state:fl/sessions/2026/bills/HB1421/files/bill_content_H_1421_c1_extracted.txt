CS/HB 1421 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to cattle grazing on state land;
3 amending s. 253.034, F.S.; requiring land managing
4 entities to identify existing grazable lands and
5 consider whether leasing portions of such land to
6 private entities for cattle grazing is appropriate
7 based on certain goals; requiring land managing
8 entities to consider certain information when
9 determining such appropriateness; requiring lands
10 determined to be appropriate for cattle grazing to be
11 described in the land management plan; requiring land
12 managing entities to allow such lands to be leased for
13 such purpose; prohibiting cattle grazing leases from
14 allowing the conversion of native wildlife habitat to
15 improved pasture; requiring land managing entities to
16 include in the land management plan an explanation for
17 a determination that no portions of such land are
18 appropriate for cattle grazing; amending s. 369.252,
19 F.S.; revising requirements for a Fish and Wildlife
20 Conservation Commission program to control invasive
21 plants; providing applicability; providing an
22 effective date.
