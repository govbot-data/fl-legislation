F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 781 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to Medicaid coverage of blood pressure
3 monitors and cuffs; creating s. 409.90631, F.S.;
4 defining the term "blood pressure monitor and cuff;"
5 requiring the Agency for Health Care Administration to
6 provide coverage for blood pressure monitors and cuffs
7 under the Medicaid pharmacy benefit for certain
8 persons; providing requirements for coverage;
9 providing that coverage of blood pressure monitors and
10 cuffs includes the cost of any necessary repairs or
11 replacement parts; providing coverage qualification
12 requirements for Medicaid recipients; requiring the
13 agency to seek federal approval for implementation of
14 coverage and to include the rate impact in the
15 Medicaid managed medical assistance program and long-
16 term care managed care program rates beginning on a
17 specified date; providing an effective date.
18
19 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
