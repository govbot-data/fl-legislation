1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the neurofibromatosis research

17 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

19 Section 1. Section 381.994, Florida Statutes, is created
20 to read:
21 381.994 Neurofibromatosis Disease Grant Program—
22 (1)(a) There is created within the Department of Health
23 the Neurofibromatosis Disease Grant Program. The purpose of the
24 program is to advance the progress of research and cures for
25 neurofibromatosis by awarding grants through a competitive,

52 (b) To ensure appropriate and fair evaluation of grant
53 applications based on scientific merit, the department shall
54 appoint peer review panels of independent, scientifically
55 qualified individuals to review the scientific merit of each
