HR 8117 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating October 18-24, 2026, as
3 "Spina Bifida Awareness Week" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, neural tube defects, including spina bifida and
6 anencephaly, occur early in pregnancy and result in lifelong
7 disability, complex medical needs, and public health costs, and
8 WHEREAS, folic acid intake before and during early
9 pregnancy is a scientific intervention to prevent neural tube
10 defects, and
11 WHEREAS, despite national fortification efforts, Florida
12 continues to experience an average of 2.5 cases of spina bifida
13 without anencephalus per 10,000 live births, with some counties
14 reporting higher rates, and
15 WHEREAS, fortification gaps in commonly consumed foods
16 contribute to ongoing disparities in folic acid intake and birth
17 outcomes, and
18 WHEREAS, adequate folic acid intake is an important factor
19 for healthy pregnancies and is linked to the prevention of
20 neural tube defects, including spina bifida and anencephaly, and
21 WHEREAS, Florida's current rate of neural tube defects has
22 an impact on public health, with each affected child often
23 requiring lifelong medical care, assistive services, and family
24 support that significantly impacts quality of life, and
