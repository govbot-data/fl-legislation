HB 773 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to municipal utilities; creating s.
3 366.16, F.S.; authorizing a municipality to transfer a
4 portion of its public utility earnings to its general
5 fund for specified purposes; setting forth the
6 percentages of such transfers based on whether the
7 public utilities are supplied to customers located
8 within or outside the municipality; requiring that
9 such percentages be established or reestablished by
10 local referendum, subject to majority vote; providing
11 an exception to percentage restrictions in the event
12 of a disaster or emergency; authorizing such
13 percentages to be established by passage of a
14 resolution, by four-fifths vote; requiring such
15 percentages to be repaid to the general fund within a
16 specified time period; prohibiting a municipality from
17 transferring a portion of its public utility earnings
18 in certain instances; prohibiting a municipality from
19 receiving certain state funds if such a prohibited
20 transfer is effected; requiring specified disclosure
21 in the budget and financial report of a municipality;
22 providing an effective date.

26 Section 1. Section 366.16, Florida Statutes, is created to
27 read:
28 366.16 Public utility earnings transfers by a
29 municipality; percentage of transfers; prohibition; disclosure
30 requirement.-
31 (1) A municipality or an agency thereof that is regulated
32 by this chapter pursuant to s. 366.04 may transfer a portion of
33 its public utility earnings to the general fund of the
34 municipality for public utility purposes.
35 (2) If the public utilities are supplied to customers
36 located within the municipality, the percentage of transfers
37 authorized by subsection (1) shall be in a fixed amount not to
38 exceed 10 percent of the municipality's or agency's general
39 fund. The amount shall be stated as a percentage of the
40 municipality's or agency's general fund that may be supported by
41 transfers.
42 (3) If the public utilities are supplied to customers
43 located outside the municipality, the percentage of transfers
44 authorized by subsection (1) shall be in a variable amount not
45 to exceed 10 percent of the municipality's or agency's general
46 fund. The amount shall be based on the percentage of customers
47 located outside the municipal boundaries and shall decrease as
48 the percentage of customers located outside the municipal
49 boundaries increases.
