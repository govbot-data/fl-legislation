35 Section 1. Section 282.31855, Florida Statutes, is created
36 to read:
37 282.31855 Local Government Cybersecurity Protection
38 Program.—
39 (1) The Local Government Cybersecurity Protection Program
40 is created within the Florida Digital Service, to be
41 administered by the Florida Digital Service. The purpose of the
42 grant program is to assist eligible local governments in
43 mitigating and defending against cybersecurity threats,
44 including, but not limited to, ransomware incidents.
45 (2) Under the grant program, the Florida Digital Service
46 shall enter into data-sharing agreements with local governments
47 as necessary to facilitate the collection, analysis, and
48 exchange of security-related information to support the
49 detection, prevention, and response to cybersecurity incidents
50 consistent with s. 282.318.
