26 to read:
27 282.31855 Local Government Cybersecurity Protection
28 Program.—
29 (1) The Local Government Cybersecurity Protection Program
30 is created within Florida State University, to be administered
31 by Florida State University. The purpose of the grant program is
32 to assist eligible local governments in mitigating and defending
33 against cybersecurity threats, including, but not limited to,
34 ransomware incidents.
35 (2) Under the grant program, Florida State University
36 shall enter into data-sharing agreements with local governments
37 and the Florida Digital Service necessary to facilitate the
38 collection, analysis, and exchange of security-related
39 information to support the detection, prevention, and response
40 to cybersecurity incidents consistent with s. 282.318.
41 (3)(a) Florida State University shall administer the grant
42 program based on objective eligibility and evaluation criteria
43 to provide information technology commodities and services
44 directly to local governments for the purpose of developing and
45 enhancing cybersecurity risk management programs consistent with
46 s. 282.3185.
47 (b) Florida State University shall contract for
48 information technology commodities and services and shall award
49 such commodities and services to local governments eligible
50 under the grant program criteria.
