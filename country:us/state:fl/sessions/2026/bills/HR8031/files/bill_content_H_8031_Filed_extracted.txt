HR 8031 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating May 2026 as "Haitian Heritage
3 Month" in the State of Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, Haitian Heritage Month is celebrated by the
6 Haitian diaspora around the world to celebrate Haitian heritage
7 and culture, and
8 WHEREAS, as educators, authors, entrepreneurs, community
9 leaders, activists, athletes, artists, musicians, and
10 politicians, Haitian Americans have made their mark and have
11 contributed to the betterment and diversity of this state and
12 the nation, and
13 WHEREAS, Haitians fought in the American Revolution to
14 support independence efforts from the British, and
15 WHEREAS, on January 1, 1804, Haiti declared and won her
16 independence from the French, led by revolutionaries to include
17 Toussaint de Bréda (also known as "Toussaint l'Ouverture"),
18 Jean-Jacques Dessalines, Henri Christophe, Alexandre Pétion,
19 Victoria Montou (also known as "Gran Toya" and "Adbaraya Toya"),
20 Dutty Boukman, Marie-Jeanne Lamartinière, Cécile Fatiman, and
21 Suzanne "Sanité" Bélair, and
22 WHEREAS, Haiti is the only country to be born of a
23 successful revolt of enslaved people declaring their
24 independence and affirming their humanity, becoming the first
25 independent state in Latin America and the Caribbean, and
