1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the use of driver license
3 information for fraud prevention; amending s. 322.142,
4 F.S.; authorizing the Department of Highway Safety and
5 Motor Vehicles to provide state agencies and certain
6 third parties with access to certain driver license
7 information for specified purposes with the licensee's
8 consent; authorizing identity verification service
9 providers to use department data under certain
10 conditions; requiring certain revenue be deposited
11 into the Highway Safety Operating Trust Fund;
12 prohibiting such providers from retaining certain
13 information; prohibiting a third party from storing or
14 selling certain information without the licensee's
15 consent; providing an effective date.
