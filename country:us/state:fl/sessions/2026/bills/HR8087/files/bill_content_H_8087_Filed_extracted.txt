2 A resolution designating August 9, 2026, as "Bob
3 Graham Day" in the State of Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, born on November 9, 1936, Daniel Robert "Bob"
6 Graham was raised on a cattle and dairy farm deep in the Florida
7 Everglades, attended Miami Senior High School, earned his
8 bachelor's degree in political science at the University of
9 Florida in 1959, and earned his Juris Doctor from Harvard Law
10 School in 1962, and
11 WHEREAS, after serving in both the Florida House of
12 Representatives and the Florida Senate, Bob Graham was elected
13 as Florida's 38th Governor in 1978, and his career in state
14 politics enabled him to personally witness Florida's rise from a
15 rural Southern backwater to an economic and political
16 juggernaut, and
17 WHEREAS, in Governor Graham's first term, led by First Lady
18 Adele Graham, Florida became a national leader in providing in-
19 home services to seniors, creating a statewide network of
20 services, assuring access to assistance for needy elders in the
21 metropolitan areas and to those in the most rural of Florida's
22 counties, and allowing more families to live out their days at
23 home, and
24 WHEREAS, in 1979, Governor Graham instituted the
25 Conservation and Recreation Act, the premier land acquisition
