27 395.1075 Central service technician services.—
28 (1) LEGISLATIVE PURPOSE AND INTENT.—The legislative
29 purpose for enacting this section is to ensure that every new
30 central service technician employed by a hospital or an
31 ambulatory surgical center in this state meets minimum
32 requirements for safe practice as a member of the surgical care
33 team. It is the intent of the Legislature that central service
34 technicians who fall below minimum competency or who otherwise
35 present a danger to public health and safety be prohibited from
36 practicing in this state.
37 (2) DEFINITIONS.—As used in this section, the term:
38 (a) "Central service technician" means a person who
39 decontaminates, inspects, assembles, disassembles, packages, and
40 sterilizes reusable medical instruments or devices for use in a
41 licensed facility.
42 (b) "Health care practitioner" has the same meaning as in
43 s. 456.001.
44 (3) CERTIFICATION AND CONTINUING EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS
45 FOR CENTRAL SERVICE TECHNICIANS.—
46 (a) A licensed facility shall employ or otherwise retain
47 the services of a central service technician only if such person
48 has successfully passed a nationally accredited exam for central
49 service technicians and meets at least one of the following
50 criteria:
