1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to Palm Beach County; amending chapter

10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:

12 Section 1. Section 5 of chapter 67-1876, Laws of Florida,
13 as amended, is amended to read:
14 Section 5. Certification.—
15 (A) To obtain a certificate of competency an applicant
16 shall submit an application in writing to the Board containing
17 the statement that the applicant desires the issuance of a
18 certificate and the class of certificate desired on a form
19 containing the information prescribed by the Board which shall
20 be accompanied by the fee fixed by the Board. All applicants
21 must be of good moral character and 18 years of age or older.
22 (B) The application shall contain the following:
23 (1) Evidence in the form of affidavits from former
24 employers, building officials, school officials, or other
25 persons to verify the required experience and education.
