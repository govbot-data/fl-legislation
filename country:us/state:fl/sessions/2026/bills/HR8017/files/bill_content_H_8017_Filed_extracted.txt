HR 8017 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating February 5, 2026, as "St.
3 Johns County Day" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, St. Johns County was established in 1821 and is
6 recognized as one of the first counties in the State of Florida,
7 and
8 WHEREAS, St. Johns County is home to the city of St
9 Augustine, which was established in 1565 and is the oldest
10 continuously occupied city of European origin in the United
11 States, and
12 WHEREAS, St. Johns County has a rich cultural heritage and
13 boasts numerous historical landmarks, including the location of
14 the first documented reference of a wooden watchtower in the
15 United States, which later became the St. Augustine Lighthouse;
16 the Castillo de San Marcos, the oldest masonry fortification in
17 the continental United States; and Fort Mose, the first free
18 African American community in the United States, and
19 WHEREAS, the early modernization of Florida began in St.
20 Johns County through the vision of Henry Flagler, whose
21 development of the railroad and the construction of the Hotel
22 Ponce de Leon, which is now the esteemed Flagler College, helped
23 shape the foundation of the region's growth, and
