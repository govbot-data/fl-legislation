37 Section 2. Section 383.012, Florida Statutes, is created

39 383.012 Home visit program for expectant mothers.—
40 (1) The Department of Health shall expand the current home
41 visit program to include a home visit program for expectant
42 mothers within the Healthy Start program, which shall include
43 Healthy Start Care Coordination Programs.
44 (2) The program shall:
45 (a)1. Conduct screenings for expectant mothers for
46 depression and substance abuse disorders provided by a
47 registered nurse, or licensed practical nurse licensed under
48 part I of chapter 464.
49 2. The department shall refer at-risk expectant mothers
50 for mental health treatment.

103 Section 6. Section 395.3043, Florida Statutes, is created
104 to read:
105 395.3043 Doula and midwife services.—
106 (1) As used in this section, the term:
107 (a) "Doula" means a nonmedical professional who provides
108 physical, emotional, and informational support to individuals
109 and families before, during, and after childbirth.
110 (b) "Midwife" has the same definition as in s. 467.003.

112 client during childbirth inside of a hospital.
