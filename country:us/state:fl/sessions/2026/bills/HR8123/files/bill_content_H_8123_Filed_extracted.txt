HR 8123 2026
1 House Resolution
2 A resolution designating November 2026 as "Pancreatic
3 Cancer Awareness Month" in Florida.
4
5 WHEREAS, pancreatic cancer typically develops when cells in
6 the pancreas mutate and multiply, forming a tumor, and
7 WHEREAS, symptoms often appear only in advanced stages,
8 pancreatic cancer is difficult to detect early, contributing to
9 its ranking as one of the deadliest cancers in the United
10 States, and
11 WHEREAS, early symptoms of pancreatic cancer may include
12 loss of appetite, weight loss, jaundice, dark-colored urine, and
13 pain and swelling in an arm or leg which may be caused by a
14 blood clot, and
15 WHEREAS, while there is no guaranteed way to prevent
16 pancreatic cancer, lifestyle changes such as avoiding tobacco
17 use, maintaining a healthy weight, engaging in regular physical
18 activity, eating a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables,
19 and managing diabetes can significantly reduce a person's risk
20 for developing the disease, and
21 WHEREAS, it is estimated that in 2025, 67,440 people were
22 diagnosed with pancreatic cancer, and
23 WHEREAS, of all major cancers, pancreatic cancer has one of
24 the highest mortality rates and is among the leading causes of
25 cancer-related death in the United States, and
