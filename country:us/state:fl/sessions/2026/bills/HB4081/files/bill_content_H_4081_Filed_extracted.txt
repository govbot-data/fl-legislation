9 WHEREAS, Apalachicola Bay is one of the most productive
10 estuarine systems in the Northern Hemisphere, boasting a rich
11 and diverse ecosystem that supports a wide array of habitats and
12 wildlife, and
13 WHEREAS, Apalachicola Bay serves as a significant nursery
14 ground for the Gulf of America, nurturing various species of
15 fish and shellfish, and

18 Gulf of America spend a portion of their life cycle in estuarine
19 waters like Apalachicola Bay, and
20 WHEREAS, Apalachicola Bay supports a local fishing industry
21 worth $14-16 million annually, which in turn directly supports
22 up to 85 percent of the local population, and
23 WHEREAS, untreated or inadequately treated wastewater from
24 old, failing, poorly maintained or overloaded septic systems on
25 St. George Island is causing excessive nutrients, disease-
