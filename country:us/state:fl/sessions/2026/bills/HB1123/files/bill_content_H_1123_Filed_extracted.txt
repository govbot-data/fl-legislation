HB 1123 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to a pilot program for students with
3 reading deficiencies; creating s. 1008.251, F.S.;
4 creating the First Grade Summer Reading Immersion
5 Pilot Program for a specified period of time;
6 providing the purpose of the program; providing
7 requirements for a student to be deemed at risk of
8 having a reading deficiency; providing Department of
9 Education and school district duties; requiring the
10 department to provide a report to the Legislature upon
11 completion of the pilot program; providing
12 requirements for such report; providing for the future
13 repeal of the program; providing an effective date.
14
15 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
16
17 Section 1. Section 1008.251, Florida Statutes, is created
18 to read:
19 1008.251 The First Grade Summer Reading Immersion Pilot
20 Program.—Beginning with the 2026-2027 school year, the First
21 Grade Summer Reading Immersion Pilot Program is created for a
22 period of 3 school years. The purpose of the program is to
23 assist school districts in providing a summer reading immersion
24 program for students who completed grade 1 and who are at risk
25 of having a reading deficiency or exhibit a substantial
