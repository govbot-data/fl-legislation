F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
CS/HB 461 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to volunteering at polling locations;
3 amending s. 97.0291, F.S.; providing that specified
4 high school students who volunteer to assist poll
5 workers are not subject to provisions prohibiting
6 certain agencies and state and local officials from
7 soliciting, accepting, or otherwise using private
8 funds or certain personal services for election-
9 related expenses; providing an effective date.
10
11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Section 97.0291, Florida Statutes, is amended
14 to read:
15 97.0291 Prohibition on use of private funds for election-
16 related expenses.—
17 (1) An No agency or a state or local official responsible
18 for conducting elections, including, but not limited to, a
19 supervisor of elections, may not solicit, accept, use, or
20 dispose of any donation in the form of money, grants, property,
21 or personal services from an individual or a nongovernmental
22 entity for the purpose of funding any type of expenses related
23 to election administration, including, but not limited to, voter
24 education, voter outreach, voter registration programs, or the
25 cost of any litigation related to election administration.
