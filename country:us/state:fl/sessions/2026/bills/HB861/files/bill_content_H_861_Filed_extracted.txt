1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to grandparent visitation rights;
3 amending s. 752.011, F.S.; authorizing a grandparent
4 of a minor child to petition a court for court-ordered
5 visitation with the child in certain circumstances;
6 making technical changes; reenacting ss. 752.015 and
7 752.071, F.S., relating to mediation of visitation
8 disputes and the effect of adoption by a stepparent or
9 close relative, respectively, to incorporate the
10 amendment made to s. 752.011, F.S., in references
11 thereto; providing an effective date.

178 Section 2. For the purpose of incorporating the amendment
179 made by this act to section 752.011, Florida Statutes, in a
180 reference thereto, section 752.015, Florida Statutes, is
181 reenacted to read:
182 752.015 Mediation of visitation disputes.—It is the public
183 policy of this state that families resolve differences over
184 grandparent visitation within the family. It is the further
185 public policy of this state that, when families are unable to
186 resolve differences relating to grandparent visitation, the
187 family participate in any formal or informal mediation services
188 that may be available. If families are unable to resolve
189 differences relating to grandparent visitation and a petition is
190 filed pursuant to s. 752.011, the court shall, if such services
191 are available in the circuit, refer the case to family mediation
192 in accordance with the Florida Family Law Rules of Procedure.
