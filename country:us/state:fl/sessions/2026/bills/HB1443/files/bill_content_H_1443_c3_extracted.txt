1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Parkinson's disease registry;
3 creating ss. 458.352, 459.075, and 464.0124 F.S.;
4 requiring physicians and advanced practice registered
5 nurses to report certain information to the
6 Parkinson's disease registry; providing limited
7 liability for physicians and advanced practice
8 registered nurses under certain circumstances;
9 amending s. 1004.4352, F.S.; requiring the President
10 of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of
11 Representatives to each appoint one member to the
12 Parkinson's Disease Research Board; revising
13 membership qualifications and terms; revising report
14 requirements; requiring the Institute for Parkinson's
15 Disease within the University of South Florida,
16 subject to appropriation, to establish and maintain a
17 statewide Parkinson's disease registry; providing
18 requirements for such registry; requiring the
19 institute to create and maintain a Parkinson's disease
20 registry-specific website; providing requirements for
21 such website; providing an effective date.
