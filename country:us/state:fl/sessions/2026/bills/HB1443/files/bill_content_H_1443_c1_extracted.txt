1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Parkinson's disease registry;
3 creating ss. 458.352 and 459.075, F.S.; requiring
4 physicians to report certain information to the

6 liability for physicians under certain circumstances;
7 amending s. 1004.4352, F.S.; authorizing the President
8 of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of
9 Representatives to each appoint one member to the
10 Parkinson's Disease Research Board; requiring the
11 board, subject to appropriation, to contract with a
12 private entity to establish and maintain a statewide
13 Parkinson's disease registry; providing requirements
14 for such registry and private entity; providing an
15 effective date.
