HB 1443 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to the Parkinson's disease registry;
3 amending s. 1004.4352, F.S.; defining the term "health
4 care provider"; authorizing the President of the
5 Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives
6 to each appoint one member to the Parkinson's Disease
7 Research Board; requiring the board, subject to
8 appropriation, to contract with a private entity to
9 establish and maintain a statewide Parkinson's disease
10 registry; providing requirements for such registry and
11 private entity; providing limited liability for health
12 care providers under certain circumstances; providing
13 an effective date.
14
15 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
16
17 Section 1. Paragraph (b) of subsection (4) of section
18 1004.4352, Florida Statutes, is amended, paragraph (c) is added
19 to subsection (3), and subsection (5) is added to that section,
20 to read:
21 1004.4352 Parkinson's disease research.—
22 (3) DEFINITIONS.—As used in this section, the term:
23 (c) "Health care provider" means a physician licensed
24 under chapter 458 or chapter 459.

26 (b) The Parkinson's Disease Research Board is established
27 to direct the operations of the consortium. The board shall be
28 composed of members representing each participating university
29 or academic medical center, appointed by the president or chief
30 executive officer of each participating university or academic
31 medical center, and the President of the Senate and the Speaker
32 of the House of Representatives shall each appoint one member.
33 Board members must have experience in a variety of scientific
34 fields, including, but not limited to, neurology, psychology,
35 nutrition, and genetics. Members shall be appointed to 4-year
36 terms and may be reappointed to serve additional terms. The
37 chair shall be elected by the board from among its members to
38 serve a 2-year term. The board shall meet at least semiannually
39 at the call of the chair or, in his or her absence or
40 incapacity, the vice chair. Four members constitute a quorum. A
41 majority vote of the members present is required for all actions
42 of the board. The board may prescribe, amend, and repeal a
43 charter governing the manner in which it conducts its business.
44 Board members shall serve without compensation, but are entitled
45 to receive reimbursement for travel expenses by the consortium
46 or the organization he or she represents in accordance with s.
47 112.061.
