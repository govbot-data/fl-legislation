ENROLLED
HB 4043 2026 Legislature
1
2 An act relating to the Citrus County Hospital Board,
3 Citrus County; requiring the district to wind down its
4 affairs and adopt a dissolution plan by a specified
5 date; repealing chs. 2011-256 and 2014-254, Laws of
6 Florida; dissolving the district on a specified date;
7 providing for the continued operation of an
8 irrevocable community trust or foundation established
9 pursuant to ch. 2014-254, Laws of Florida;
10 transferring district assets and liabilities to the
11 Board of County Commissioners of Citrus County;
12 providing effective dates.
13
14 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
15
16 Section 1. The Citrus County Hospital Board shall wind
17 down its affairs, including, but not limited to, adopting a
18 dissolution plan that includes satisfying all of its obligations
19 and indebtedness and making provisions for the use of any
20 remaining assets of the district, including, but not limited to,
21 distributions to the irrevocable community trust or foundation
22 created pursuant to chapter 2014-254, Laws of Florida. Such plan
23 shall be adopted by September 30, 2026.
24 Section 2. Effective October 1, 2026, chapters 2011-256
25 and 2014-254, Laws of Florida, are repealed.
