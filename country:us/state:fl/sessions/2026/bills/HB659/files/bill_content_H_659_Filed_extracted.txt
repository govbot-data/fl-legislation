F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 659 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to interactions with artificial
3 intelligence; creating s. 501.172, F.S.; providing
4 definitions; requiring operators of companion chatbot
5 platforms to display specified information on any
6 application, browser, or format used to access
7 companion chatbots on the platform and to prevent such
8 chatbots from interacting with users unless specified
9 protocols are maintained; requiring such operators to
10 verify the age of users and to take specified actions
11 for users who are minors; requiring such operators to
12 file specified reports with the Department of Legal
13 Affairs; providing that a violation constitutes an
14 unfair or deceptive act or practice; providing for
15 civil actions; providing construction; providing an
16 effective date.
17
18 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
