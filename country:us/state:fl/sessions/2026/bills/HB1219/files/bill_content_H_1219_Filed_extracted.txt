8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. Section 258.603, Florida Statutes, is created
11 to read:
12 258.603 Andrew "Red" Harris Shoal designated.—
13 (1) Shoal S13 within the Jupiter Inlet is designated as
14 the "Andrew "Red" Harris Shoal."

16 to erect suitable markers designating the "Andrew "Red" Harris
17 Shoal" as described in subsection (1).
18 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
