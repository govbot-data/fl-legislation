F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
CS/HB 1219 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to waterbody designations; creating s.
3 258.603, F.S.; designating the Andrew "Red" Harris
4 Spoil Island; directing the Department of
5 Environmental Protection to erect suitable markers;
6 providing an effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
9
10 Section 1. Section 258.603, Florida Statutes, is created
11 to read:
12 258.603 Andrew "Red" Harris Spoil Island designated.—
13 (1) SI3, a mangrove island adjacent to the Intracoastal
14 Waterway within Jupiter Sound, is designated as the "Andrew
15 "Red" Harris Spoil Island."
16 (2) The Department of Environmental Protection is directed
17 to erect suitable markers designating the "Andrew "Red" Harris
18 Spoil Island" as described in subsection (1).
19 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
