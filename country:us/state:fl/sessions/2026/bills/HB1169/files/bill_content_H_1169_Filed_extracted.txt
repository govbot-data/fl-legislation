HB 1169 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to enforcement of the Florida Building
3 Code; amending s. 553.80, F.S.; providing that a local
4 government is not eligible for additional state funds
5 if it has been subject to a legislative committee's
6 audit within a specified timeframe or if it fails to
7 submit an affirmation to the legislative delegation;
8 requiring each legislative committee to report such
9 local governments to the presiding officers and the
10 appropriations chairs; providing an effective date.
