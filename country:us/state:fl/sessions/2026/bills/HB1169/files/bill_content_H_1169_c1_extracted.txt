1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to enforcement of the Florida Building
3 Code; amending s. 553.80, F.S.; removing the authority
4 of a local government to use excess funds to pay for
5 the construction of certain buildings or structures;
6 providing that a local government is not eligible to
7 receive state funds if it has been subject to a
8 legislative committee's audit within a specified
9 timeframe or if it fails to submit a specified
10 affirmation to the legislative delegation; requiring
11 each legislative committee to report such local
12 governments to the presiding officers and the chairs
13 of the legislative appropriations committees;
14 providing an effective date.
