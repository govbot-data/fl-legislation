F L O R I D A H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S
HB 7011 2026
1 A bill to be entitled
2 An act relating to a review under the Open Government
3 Sunset Review Act; amending s. 597.0042, F.S., which
4 provides an exemption from public record requirements
5 for certain aquaculture records held by the Department
6 of Agriculture and Consumer Services; removing the
7 scheduled repeal of the exemption; providing an
8 effective date.
9
10 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
11
12 Section 1. Subsection (4) of section 597.0042, Florida
13 Statutes, is amended to read:
14 597.0042 Public records exemptions; aquaculture records.—
15 (4) This section is subject to the Open Government Sunset
16 Review Act in accordance with s. 119.15 and shall stand repealed
17 on October 2, 2026, unless reviewed and saved from repeal
18 through reenactment by the Legislature.
19 Section 2. This act shall take effect upon becoming a law.
