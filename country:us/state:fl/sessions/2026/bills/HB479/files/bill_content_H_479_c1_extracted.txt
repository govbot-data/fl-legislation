2 An act relating to land and water management; creating

11 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
12
13 Section 1. Section 373.027, Florida Statutes, is created
14 to read:
15 373.027 Prohibition on counties and municipalities
16 prohibiting adjacent upland activities outside a certain wetland
17 buffer.—
18 (1) A county or municipality may not adopt a law,
19 regulation, rule, policy, or ordinance that prohibits adjacent
20 upland activities that are outside a wetland buffer that is a
21 minimum width of 15 feet from the wetland and an average width
22 of 25 feet from the wetland.
23 (2) This section does not:
24 (a) Apply to an interagency or interlocal agreement
